# The prime inequality used by the original RH source

Director A4 · November 22, 2026 research session · B.C. Pacific Time (PCT, UTC−07:00)

The original proof calls a bound for **each translated prime table**. Averaging it gives the upper bound on the left of the exposition’s (M). Dividing its explicit error by the exposition’s normalization reproduces all four costs in (M). The source also fixes the order of the window and exponent quantifiers. This traces an intermediate result on the proof path. The construction of the data satisfying the arithmetic hypotheses and a kernel replay of the original development are outside this check. [Exposition, §§I.5–I.6 and II](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~mrubinst/alpoge_claude_grh/zeta-paper.pdf) · [External verification record](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/)

## Normalizing the four costs

Put $n=|H|$, $t=|T|$, $r=|T_2|$, $\delta=r/n$, $X=x^\varepsilon$, and $B=tmb\delta/2$. Let $a_*$ be the source’s upper scale for the derivative orders. For strictly positive denominators the raw error bracket $Q$ in `ch_top` satisfies the exact identity
$$
\frac{Q}{B}
=\frac{2}{\delta}\frac{a_*}{b}\left(\frac1m+\frac{m}{k}\right)
+4\frac{n}{t}\frac{1}{\delta}Xm
+2\frac{n}{t}X^2\frac{k}{n^2}.
$$
The source bounds $a_*\le 2xb$, $\delta^{-1}\le X$ and $n/t\le 2X^{1/8}$. At $X\ge1$, replacing the last bound by $2X$ gives
$$
Q/B\le 8 X^3\left(x/m+xm/k+m+k/n^2\right).
$$
The per-translate estimate includes another factor $X^{e_{\rm Div}+e_{\rm Loss}+3}$. Thus the normalized mean is bounded by eight times the four costs times $X^{e_{\rm Div}+e_{\rm Loss}+6}$. The fixed factor eight can be absorbed by increasing the exponent once $x^\varepsilon\ge8$. The calculation needs positive table sizes and keeps the factor of two in $B$. [Source bracket and exponent](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ErrorFormsWeakWindowsFalse.lean#L133) · [Density bounds](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/SChain.lean#L53) · [Local Lean checks](PrimeInequality.lean)

For the zero-free implication, the family exponents and admissible upper bound for $\varepsilon$ are chosen before the small $\varepsilon$. Within that choice the family, constants and cutoff may depend on $\varepsilon$. Eventually the estimate holds for every $x$ in the family’s windows. Those windows contain $[Y,Y^{3/2}]$ for arbitrarily large $Y$. The exponent entering the final character estimate is $1/2+(e_D+e_L+7)\varepsilon$. The proof takes $\varepsilon$ small enough for the real part of the particular proposed zero. A single numerical table, one window or a bound at one fixed positive $\varepsilon$ does not supply these quantifiers. **Disposition.** The source call, sign, normalization and four error terms agree at this interface.
