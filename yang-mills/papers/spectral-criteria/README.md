# Spectral criteria and conditional infrared estimates for four-dimensional lattice Yang–Mills theory

Kirtus Justus, Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada. Part of the research program [From Prephysical Hypotheses to Physical Models](https://github.com/KirtusJ/From-Prephysical-Hypotheses-to-Physical-Models).

The paper proves a spectral criterion for a mass gap, an inequality that reduces one lattice refinement step to three estimates, and one of those estimates for SU(2). The other two are open. It does not prove a mass gap, and it has not been peer reviewed.
