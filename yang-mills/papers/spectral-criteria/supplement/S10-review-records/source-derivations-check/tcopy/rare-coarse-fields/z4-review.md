At $n=1$ the right side of lower line 124 equals $2(1)(1/2)=1$. There is no interior point to be drawn in the density step at line 135. Requiring $n\ge2$ repairs the corollary. The earlier flow lemma assumes the congestion bound explicitly. The final path crosses a segment of length $\pi$ in steps of at most $d/\sqrt\beta$ and satisfies the repaired hypothesis. **2. The cutoff witnessing the E3 error rate.** The choice $L=\sqrt{\log\beta}$ at line 958 makes the tails vanish, but the specific E3 certificate evaluated at this $L$ cannot have the rate quoted at line 591. Write $t=\sqrt\beta\,\theta$ and $\Lambda=\sup|\Psi'|>0$. For large $\beta$ the interval $[L+1/L,L+2/L]$ lies above $\sqrt\beta\arcsin(L/\sqrt\beta)=L+O(L^3/\beta)$. The density of $t$ there is at least $c\,t^2e^{-t^2/2}$. This follows from the Haar Jacobian, $\sin\theta\ge2\theta/\pi$ on the interval, $1-\cos\theta\le\theta^2/2$, and $Z e^{-\beta}=O(\beta^{-3/2})$ by the reverse quadratic bound on the whole sphere. Restricting the first positive term of $Q$ in line 566 to that interval gives
$$Q\ge c' L\beta^{-1/2}e^{\Lambda L}.$$
Its ratio to $L^3/\sqrt\beta$ tends to infinity. At a fixed valley point in the tube $w=0$, the outer coefficient in line 587 is strictly positive and independent of $\beta$, the flat gap is zero, and the inner term is at least $1-O(\beta^{-2/3})$ by the normalization estimate. Thus cancellation cannot restore this *particular* certificate's rate.
