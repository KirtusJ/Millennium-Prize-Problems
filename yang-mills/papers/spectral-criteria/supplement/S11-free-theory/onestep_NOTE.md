# Note on the three one-step runs

- `onestep_result.txt`: predicate O2 FAILED. With sums of `r` links along a line, `c_r` grows like `r²`. Kept.
- `onestep2_result.txt`: predicate met. Box average of the plaquette field; `c_r/r` is 0.46 to 0.72 at the lowest axial momentum for `r` = 2 to 8, increasing.
- `onestep3_result.txt`: P1 met; P2 FAILED as written and is kept. P2 asked that the values at `r` = 6 and 8 at the lowest axial momentum differ by less than 3 per cent; they differ by 3.5 per cent, and in the favourable direction (0.1733 to 0.1673, decreasing). The predicate was written to detect continued growth and its tolerance was two-sided. What the run shows: with the triangular weight the supremum over coarse momenta is no longer at the lowest axial momentum; it is 0.759326, 1.039043, 1.337221, 1.953024, 2.579001 for `r` = 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, the same at sides `4r` and `8r`, and equal to six figures to `1/ω(π/r, 0, 0)`, the inverse energy of the lowest mode at the edge of the coarse Brillouin zone (checked by direct evaluation: 0.759326, 1.039043, 1.337221, 1.953024, 2.579001). So `c_r/r → 1/π`: in units of the coarse mesh the constant is bounded, and it is the value forced by the resolution of the coarse lattice. This identification was made after the run; it is an observation at the sizes computed, not a proof.
- `onestep4_result.txt` (steps of the proof of the bound `c_r ≤ 1056 r` for the twice-averaged block plaquette field): Q1, Q2, Q3 met; Q4 FAILED and is kept. Q4 asserted that the explicit combination of series is below 1055; it is 1055.163 (the value 1055 came from hand arithmetic with rounded terms). The proof therefore gives `ρ(k) ≤ 1055.17 r`, and with `1/ω(π/r,0,0) ≤ r/(2 arsinh 1) = 0.5673 r` the stated bound `c_r ≤ 1056 r` stands (1055.74). The largest `ρ(k)/r` actually found is 1.70, so the proof loses a factor of about 600 against its own intermediate quantity, and the computed `c_r/r` is 0.32 to 0.38.
- `linesum_result.txt` (the lower bound for line-sum blocking proposed by a reviewer): S1, S2, S4 met; S3 FAILED as written and is kept. Its first half holds (every computed constant is at least the bound). Its second half asked that the computed constants exceed the bound by less than 2 per cent at every factor; that is so at `r` = 8 (0.7 per cent) but not at small `r` (59 per cent at `r` = 2), where modes other than the one in the trial vector contribute. The 2 per cent was my guess, not part of the reviewer's statement.
- A second reviewer pointed out that the trial vector of Proposition E.6 discards the contribution of the high aliases themselves; keeping it gives the limiting value `S_r + (Σ' 1/(ω sinh ω))/(Σ' 1/sinh ω)`, which in units of the coarse mesh is 0.7246, 1.1199, 1.5733, 2.5356, 3.5223, 7.5262 for `r` = 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 16 (recomputed in the session from that formula; it reproduces the reviewer's six values). This is why the 2 per cent predicate failed at small `r`, and it agrees with the constants of `onestep_result.txt` (0.7241, 1.1142, 1.5604, 2.4991, 3.4502 at side `1024 r`, slightly below the limit of zero momentum).
