The root guest requested the spectrum worker, which requested the limit worker. Each ran in a separate PCP OS boot. The host supervisor checked the loader’s kernel measurement, worker ELF digest, child request, successful exit, register head and kernel self-test completion. It assembled the source from the guests’ serial output. Independent mathematical review remains a separate step.

Lean checks the scalar spread and four-face implication, the finite symmetric row-sum inequality, the numerical second-moment ceiling, mass interpolation for a finite measure, the variance and Rayleigh estimates, and the low-energy integral and scalar estimate for weighted escape. The sphere integration by parts, localization of the mixed commutator, geometric incidence, and physical interpretation are analytic. Changing 25 to 24 in the spread inequality failed Lean. The choice k = 4/3, y = 6/25 satisfies the premise with equality and refutes 24. Removing the row symmetry also failed. For the matrix with rows (1,0),(1,0), unit row sums and coefficients (2,1), the claimed bound becomes 6 ≤ 5.
