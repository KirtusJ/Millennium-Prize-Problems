## The exact score and the part of the action it misses

Fix a faithful unitary representation of a positive-dimensional compact Lie group. Write $U$ for its matrix, $N$ for its dimension, $d$ for the group dimension, and $P$ for orthogonal projection of the real matrix space onto $\mathfrak h=d\rho(\mathfrak g)$. Use the scalar product $\langle A,B\rangle=\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(A^*B)/N$ and its norm $|\cdot|$. Define $Y=\beta(1-\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}U/N)$ and $F(U)=\sqrt\beta P(U-I)$. For $X\in\mathfrak h$,

$$
 \left.\frac{d}{dt}Y(e^{tX/\sqrt\beta}U)\right|_{t=0}=\langle X,F(U)\rangle,
 \qquad 2Y=|F(U)|^2+\beta |(I-P)(U-I)|^2.
$$

The first identity uses $X^*=-X$ and $\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}X=0$. The second follows from unitarity and orthogonal projection. The normal component has norm bounded by $C_\rho|U-I|^2$ globally. Near identity use $U=e^Z$, $Z\in\mathfrak h$, $|Z|\le C_\rho|U-I|$ and the second-order Taylor remainder. Off that neighborhood the chordal distance is bounded below by compactness and faithfulness. This also covers disconnected groups. Thus $0\le 2Y-|F|^2\le C_\rho Y^2/\beta$. A uniform fourth moment of $Y$ gives an $O_\rho(\beta^{-1})$ error in $L^2$. In the original SU(2) defining representation the discarded term is exactly $Y^2/\beta$.

For inversion one has $P(U^*-I)=-P(U-I)$, since their sum is Hermitian. Unitary conjugation by a group element preserves $\mathfrak h$ and commutes with $P$. Thus reflection of a re-rooted face gives the exact odd sign needed for each real color component. In the strip Gram argument use real coefficients and the real part of the normalized character. For real coefficients the resulting quadratic form is the real part of the positive complex Gram form. This retains $1-\operatorname{Re}\mathbb E\operatorname{Tr}(U_{1\times l})/N\le l\,\mathbb E(Y_p/\beta)$.

The scaled direct derivative of $F$ at the identity generator insertion is $P(XU)$, which differs from $X$ by $P(X(U-I))$. Its norm is at most $\|X\|_{\rm op}|U-I|$. The path derivative is an infinitesimal conjugation, bounded by $2\|X\|_{\rm op}|F|/\sqrt\beta$ per traversal. In the ordered cube word first expand the product about $I$, then apply $P$ to the ambient linear sum. All products with at least two deficits have expected norm $O_\rho(\beta^{-1})$ by Cauchy–Schwarz and bounded unitaries. These estimates justify the score, derivative, and inversion replacements in the Ward recursion. The color contractions of half the squared norm give $dG^2/2$ for two Gaussian vectors with cross-covariance $G I_d$. Constants can depend on the fixed representation. The action tail remains a separate input for that representation and state class. The previously deposited SU(2) quantitative theorems have their original scope.
