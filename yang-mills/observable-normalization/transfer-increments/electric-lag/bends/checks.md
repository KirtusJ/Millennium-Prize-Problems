# Verification of the square response

The three generations ran as separate PCP OS guests. The host followed the child requests emitted in the first two guests. The parent counted unit faces sharing one or two edges on seven periodic grids, with coarse sides two through eight fine edges and four coarse periods. The child enumerated all 512 shared and private quaternion-path triples and checked the Haar singlet projection and the two norms. The grandchild tested the finite propagation sum for coincident and distinct integer eigenvalues. The representation-theoretic decomposition for continuous SU(2), the vacuum subtraction and the optimization over fine readings are written proofs. [Derivation](README.md)

Each guest recorded 47 events in each run. Lean 4.19.0 with Mathlib `c44e0c8ee63ca166450922a373c7409c5d26b00b` checked six declarations covering the incidence and norm arithmetic, the propagation sum and the scalar optimization. The axiom reports list only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Two mutations with the wrong incidence count and the wrong triplet slope sign failed to compile.

The [independent Python controls](check.py) include three calculations.

* On continuous SU(2), radial quadrature of the Wilson density and a direction rule exact to quadratic order check the shared-path convolution as a matrix on quaternion coordinates. The 192 comparisons cover two path lengths and temporal couplings 0.5, 2, 8 and 24.
* On the 512-state quaternion graph, a central keep-or-redraw chain permits a direct finite difference with a recomputed transfer vacuum and conditional expectation. The graph contains just the two loops and their common path; the conditional algebra is generated by the coarse trace. Both predicted component derivatives agreed within $10^{-9}$ at lag three for one and two common edges. In this finite chain the centered shared-path multiplier is $q^d$. It differs from the adjoint Wilson multiplier $a^d$. Replacing $q^2$ by $q$ for the corner or dropping the triplet normalization caused these tests to fail.
* The fixed-lag, scaled Wilson slope at perimeter twelve and temporal coupling 100,000 was 0.08303315, against the predicted limit 0.08305815.
