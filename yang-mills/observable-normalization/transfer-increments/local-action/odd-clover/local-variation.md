# A strictly positive ultraviolet contribution

Write $q=P/\beta$ for the unscaled site polynomial. Choose three positive links with (base, direction)
$$
 (0,0),\quad (0,1),\quad (e_2,3).
$$
At identity values of the other links set these three to $\exp(u\mathbf i),\exp(v\mathbf j),\exp(w\mathbf k)$. The coefficient of $uvw$ in the total of $q$ over all affected sites is $-1/16$.

There are three excited directions and only two faces in any summand of $q$. The third link cannot share a face with either of the first two. Their planes would be 03 or 13, but the bases differ in their 2-coordinate. Thus the first two links must occupy one 01 face, necessarily the square with lower corner zero. The other face is in plane 23 with coordinates 0 and 1 fixed at zero. Their common root must therefore be the origin. The third link then fixes the positive 23 square. In the based positive 01 word the two nonidentity factors are $\exp(u\mathbf i)\exp(-v\mathbf j)$, in that order. Its component odd in both variables is $-\mathbf k\sin u\sin v$. The 23 word equals $\exp(w\mathbf k)$. The part of the entire charge odd in all three angles is therefore exactly $-\sin u\sin v\sin w/16$.

The [grandchild guest](worker.c) checks the coefficient independently. It expands each oriented word modulo $(u^2,v^2,w^2)$ by ordered subsequences, records signed imaginary components by variable mask, and contracts complementary masks and planes. With both clover denominators removed, the origin gives $-1$ and the other 255 sites of $\{-1,0,1,2\}^4$ give zero. Every possible root must be on a face containing the first link. The [finite-difference test](check_odd.py) uses full unit quaternions in four real coordinates.

## Conditional variation on a separated family of triples

Inside $B_N$ take translates of this triple with base points on a mesh of spacing 12 and distance at least four from the boundary. At least $c_0 N^4$ fit for large $N$. No action plaquette and no site charge monomial involves links of two triples. Given all the other links, the triples are independent by the finite-range Wilson Gibbs specification. The latter passes from the periodic measures to any local limit since integration with the normalized kernel for a fixed finite set preserves continuity on boundary links. At fixed $N$ the conditional charge sum is a constant plus a sum of functions $F_i$, one per triple, each equal to $\beta$ times the part of the raw site sum affected by this triple. One may include full site terms on affected sites, absorbing any exterior-only terms into the constant.

For each triple take a translated box $[-4,4]^4$ around its base. Choose a fixed spanning tree of its vertices avoiding the three links. Deleting them leaves the graph connected since each has a private three-edge path around a plaquette avoiding all three. Gauge-fix the tree with transformations determined by the exterior links only. Each other exterior link needed for an affected action face or charge is now the holonomy of a fixed finite closed path in this box, up to a common frame. By a fixed plaquette filling and the triangle inequality for the bi-invariant chordal metric, its distance to identity has $L^2(\mu)$ norm at most $C/\sqrt\beta$. Indeed the distance of a face is $\sqrt{2(1-\operatorname{Re} U_p)}$ and the uniform one-face action expectation is bounded. The filling is only used for an unconditional moment bound and may use selected links. For a fixed $M$ large enough, all the finitely many participating exterior links in this frame lie within $M/\sqrt\beta$ of identity with probability at least $1/2$. Denote this exterior-measurable event by $E_i$. The argument needs only its marginal probability for each triple. [Uniform face bound](../../../../central-quotients/tangent-curvature/periodic-limit/pressure.md)

The tree gauge preserves conditional Haar measure in the three free links. Their conditional density is proportional to the exponential of minus $\beta$ times the sum of the Wilson deficits on faces meeting the triple. Choose one private face per link, respectively in plane 02 on the negative-2 side, plane 12 on the negative-2 side, and plane 23 at the third link. These three faces contain only their own selected link. Dropping the other nonnegative deficits upper-bounds the normalizer by $C\beta^{-9/2}$. In fact each resulting one-link integral is a translated Haar integral of $e^{-\beta(1-\operatorname{Re} U)}$, bounded by $C\beta^{-3/2}$ by integrating $\sin^2\theta\,e^{-\beta(1-\cos\theta)}$ for $0\le\theta\le\pi$.

Use the chart $U_\ell=\exp(\phi_\ell/\sqrt\beta)$ with each of the nine imaginary coordinates in $[-1,1]$. On $E_i$ every affected face is within $C_M/\sqrt\beta$ of identity on this chart, so the exponential weight is bounded below by a positive constant. The three Haar Jacobians together are bounded below by $c\beta^{-9/2}$. Dividing by the upper bound for the normalizer shows that the conditional probability dominates $c_M$ times uniform probability on the nine-dimensional cube. This is a lower bound on measures, independent of the triple location.

With respect to that uniform probability let $g=\phi_{1,1}\phi_{2,2}\phi_{3,3}$. Then $\int g=0$ and $\int g^2=1/27$. In the Taylor expansion in the three chart vectors, integrating against $g$ kills every monomial through degree three except this one. Its raw coefficient changes from $-1/16$ by $O_M(\beta^{-1/2})$ on $E_i$, by smooth dependence on the finitely many exterior links. Fourth derivatives are uniformly bounded on the patch. Restoring the factor $\beta$ in $F_i$ gives
$$
 \int g F_i=-\frac{1}{432\sqrt\beta}+O_M(\beta^{-1}).
$$
For any constant $b$, Cauchy–Schwarz bounds $\int(F_i-b)^2$ below by $(\int gF_i)^2/\int g^2$. Conditional minorization and the infimum over $b$ therefore give $\operatorname{Var}(F_i\mid\mathrm{exterior})\ge c_M/\beta$ on $E_i$ at sufficiently large coupling. Conditional independence adds these variances. Total variance and $\mu(E_i)\ge1/2$ give $\operatorname{Var}\sum_{B_N}P\ge c N^4/\beta$. Taking the box limit yields the asserted lower bound.
