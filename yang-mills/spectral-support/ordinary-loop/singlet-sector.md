# What ordinary trace words generate

Transport all loops at one slice to the same basepoint by prescribed lattice connectors. Put $X_A^\beta=\sqrt\beta\,\operatorname{Im} U_A$. The scalar part of the quaternion product gives exact identities at **every coupling**,
$$
 X_A^\beta\cdot X_B^\beta=\frac\beta2[W(AB^{-1})-W(AB)],\qquad
 X_A^\beta\cdot(X_B^\beta\times X_C^\beta)
     =\frac{\beta^{3/2}}2[W(ACB)-W(ABC)] .
$$
Indeed $\operatorname{Re}(ab)=a_0 b_0-\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b$. Exchanging the last two factors changes the sign of the triple product in the scalar part of $abc$. Also $2W(A)W(B)=W(AB)+W(AB^{-1})$. These identities show exactly which gauge-invariant Wilson words implement contractions at the cutoff. In particular, conjugating a whole insert by a common temporal trunk has no effect. [Lean identities](OrdinaryLoop.lean)

**Separated moments.** Write $K_{ij}$ for the one-color circulation covariance between inserts $i,j$. For the centered defects $Q_i=\tfrac12:|X_i|^2:$, Wick's formula gives $\mathbb E Q_1Q_2=3K_{12}^2/2$, and $\mathbb E Q_1Q_2Q_3=3K_{12}K_{23}K_{31}$. Normal ordering removes the same-vertex pairings. The two-vertex product has two cross-pairings; the triangle has eight. Each connected diagram forces a single common color and each vertex contributes $1/2$. A nonzero triangle distinguishes this composite from a centered Gaussian observable. For identical squares at three distinct times the Wilson-limit ratio
$$
 \frac{\mathbb E Q_1 Q_2 Q_3}
 {\sqrt{\mathbb E Q_1 Q_2\,\mathbb E Q_2 Q_3\,\mathbb E Q_3 Q_1}}
   =\sqrt{8/3}
$$
also holds for the limiting readings of the centered *unscaled defects*, since the powers of $\beta$ cancel.

For triples of contours on slices separated by $t$, set $D=\det(X_A,X_B,X_C)$ and $K=(K_{ij}(t))_{i,j=1}^3$ across the slices. Internal contractions vanish by antisymmetry in color. Expanding both alternating symbols and matching each color yields $\mathbb E D(0)D(t)=6\det K$. The mixed quadratic/cubic reading vanishes by odd total degree. Center the finite-cutoff determinant when taking its spectral measure. Its Gaussian mean is zero. [Native contraction enumeration](guest.h)

Choose three equal squares in the coordinate planes, with the same physical center. Use dyadic connectors to a common base. Crossing two different planes gives zero covariance: reflection of the coordinate normal to the first plane fixes its oriented boundary and reverses the oriented second square. The free kernel is invariant under that reflection. The diagonal entries all equal $K_R(t)>0$. Thus $C_3(t)=6K_R(t)^3$, and $C_3(t)^2/C_2(t)^3=32/3$. Here $C_3$ denotes the **two-point** connected reading of the oriented determinant. At the cutoff use $[W(ACB)-W(ABC)]/2$ for this reading and $1-W$ for the quadratic reading, centering both against the actual vacuum. Their bare powers cancel exactly from the ratio; both times may be chosen strictly positive relative to a reflection plane. The two ratios specify distinct tests of the trace sector and do not require knowledge of the bare field amplitude.

**A Hilbert space identified by the probes.** For a finite list of loops, the invariant polynomials of the corresponding color vectors under common $SO(3)$ rotations are generated by their pairwise inner products and triple determinants. This is the classical first fundamental theorem for the special orthogonal group. Complex polynomial coefficients follow by complexification. The trace identities above implement these polynomial generators exactly. Wick order at each finite mesh with the *comparison Gaussian's* equal-time covariance matrix. Every internal contraction is rotation invariant, so the resulting lower-degree polynomial counterterms are again obtainable by ordinary trace words and constants. These coefficients depend on the mesh and may diverge. At fixed mesh C4's moment theorem gives the Wick-ordered Gaussian polynomial as comparison. After the common diagonal, separated-vertex readings converge because each surviving contraction joins two different times. [Kraft and Procesi, §10.2](https://dmi.unibas.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/dmi/Personen/Kraft_Hanspeter/Classical_Invariant_Theory.pdf)

Let $H_T$ be the complex transverse vector space with norm measured by $d^3p/((2\pi)^3\,2|p|)$, and $H=H_T\otimes\mathbb C^3$ with global color rotations on the last factor. A Wick-ordered homogeneous degree $k$ probe placed time $s>0$ from the reflection plane maps to a symmetric tensor of the vectors $e^{-s|p|}j_C(p)$ and their color labels. Cross-Wick contraction gives its Fock inner product (with the $k!$ normalization on symmetrized elementary tensors). Different degrees are orthogonal. Transfer over $u>0$ multiplies a $k$-particle tensor by $\exp(-u\sum_{\ell=1}^k|p_\ell|)$. The tilt makes each contour vector square integrable although its undamped sharp-time limit may have infinite norm.

For any fixed dyadic $s>0$, the damped dyadic rectangle currents span a dense subspace of $H_T$. To check this, suppose $h\in H_T$ annihilates every such vector. The inverse Fourier transform of $e^{-s|p|}h(p)/(2|p|)$ is a smooth vector field $V$; absolute integrability of each derivative follows from Cauchy–Schwarz and the exponential. Its circulation vanishes on all dyadic coordinate rectangles. Shrinking these at dyadic points and using continuity gives $\nabla\times V=0$ everywhere. In Fourier variables $p\times h(p)=0$ for almost all $p\ne0$. Transversality also gives $p\cdot h(p)=0$, hence $h=0$. Haar averaging of elementary degree-$k$ symmetric tensors from this dense span gives a dense set of invariant tensors. On each finite span their coefficient polynomials are the invariant polynomials already realized by the trace words. This identifies the Hilbert completion of this constructed probe space, including the vacuum, as
$$
 \bigoplus_{k\ge0}\left(\operatorname{Sym}^k H\right)^{SO(3)}.
$$
There is no degree-one color singlet. Degrees two and three already see energies arbitrarily close to zero. The assertion concerns the explicitly scaled, Wick-ordered and positive-time-translated trace probes on the selected Wilson sequence. Other regulator states and other trajectories remain outside the identification. Varadarajan constructs Fock representations for abelian holonomy algebras; the construction above uses the three-color tangent field, simultaneous color singlets, and actual signed $SU(2)$ trace combinations before the limit. [Varadarajan, Phys. Rev. D 61, 104001](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.61.104001)
