A4's squared, conditionally centered temporal-link action has an explicit **joint physical energy–momentum measure** on sufficiently weak isotropic four-dimensional $SU(2)$ Wilson trajectories. With $r=1/b$, one example is $\beta=r^{65}$ and $L=\lceil r^{13}\rceil$ spatial cells per side, with the infinite-time transfer vacuum taken at every regulator. For positive time separations use the covariance multiplied by $\beta^4r^8$. Its limiting density with respect to $dE\,d^3p/(2\pi)^3$ is
$$
 \rho(E,p)=\frac{{\bf1}_{E>|p|}}{49152\pi^2}
 \left[(E^2-|p|^2)^2+\frac{8}{15}|p|^4\right].
$$
Every open set strictly inside the positive cone carries weight. At fixed nonzero momentum the density has a positive one-sided limit at the two-particle threshold. Integrating momentum gives the earlier temporal coefficient $1/(128\pi^4t^8)$. The result uses C4's growing-box Ward estimates and uniform second-moment bound. It identifies a free electric sector of a single original Wilson insertion. Construction of interacting Yang–Mills theory at a fixed nonzero renormalized scale and proof of its mass gap remain open.
