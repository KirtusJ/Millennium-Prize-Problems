# The compact calculation in one spatially twisted ensemble

Use periodic links with lengths $(6,2,2,2)$, direction 0 for time, and spatial 't Hooft flux through directions 1 and 2. Each face has $D_p=1-\eta_p\operatorname{Re} U_p$ in unit quaternion notation. The sheet factor $\eta_p=-1$ exactly on oriented 12-faces crossing both periodic seams; elsewhere it is $+1$. Equivalently one can use twisted transitions and ordinary local deficits. The sheet is part of both the action and the insertions. A useful background puts $i$ and $j$ on the respective seam links and identity elsewhere. Its compensated curvature is identity everywhere. Perturbative gauge theory has long used twists to remove gauge zero modes. ## The saddle and its measure

Positive plaquette weights force every minimum to be projectively flat. Parallel transport on the universal cover, or contracting a loop by elementary faces, leaves four cycle holonomies. Their commutation signs are the prescribed fluxes. The twisted pair must anticommute; for unit quaternions that requires two perpendicular pure imaginary unit vectors. A simultaneous conjugation sends them to $i,j$. Both remaining holonomies commute with this pair and are independently $\pm1$. Center flips of the appropriate seam links exchange the four sectors and preserve every compensated plaquette. Thus they have identical weight derivatives.

Fix a spanning tree avoiding wrapping edges. The two winding variables on the coordinate axes then occur on single chord links, denoted $H_i,H_j$. The remaining common conjugation rotates their vector parts by a common $SO(3)$ rotation. Align the first with $i$, and put the vector part of the second in the $i,j$ plane with positive $j$ component. This fixes the rotational frame locally at every minimum. Haar invariance rotates the other chord variables without changing their integration volume. The remaining measure has three types of factors in the rescaled exponential chart $\epsilon=\beta^{-1/2}$.

| Chord | Active left increment around its background | Density relative to flat coordinates, up to a source-independent constant |
|---|---|---|
| First winding variable | $X=a i$ | $\cos^2(\epsilon a)$ |
| Second winding variable | $X=bj+ck$, $r=\epsilon\sqrt{b^2+c^2}$ | $(\sin r/r)\cos r$ |
| Every other chord | All three components, $r=\epsilon\lVert X\rVert$ | $(\sin r/r)^2$ |

The first line is the Haar polar angle density near angle $\pi/2$. For the second write $H_j=\exp(Y)j$. Its $k$ component is $Y_i\operatorname{sinc}|Y|$. Imposing zero $k$ component removes one sinc factor. The azimuthal orbit about $i$ contributes the positive $j$ component $\cos|Y|$. Together with full Haar these give the second line. Thus the quadratic density corrections are $-a^2$, $-2(b^2+c^2)/3$ and $-\lVert X\rVert^2/3$ respectively. Reversing the tree preference and fixing the $j$ axis first provides another gauge computation.

Transport each signed slot of a face word to its beginning by the preceding background holonomy. For an inverse link include the inverse background before transporting the negative increment. Background adjoints are diagonal signs in the $i,j,k$ color basis. With the sheet factor removed the face is a word of exponentials of these signed vectors, even at a seam. Write $B_c$ for its additive curl in the active coordinates of color $c$. Every adjoint color changes sign on at least one spatial cycle. The twisted cochain complex at any allowed momentum is a Koszul complex with at least one nonzero difference; contraction in that direction gives an explicit homotopy. In particular the closed one-cochains are infinitesimal gauge. Tree fixing and the two winding constraints remove all of them. Equivalently the tree leaves a constant infinitesimal common conjugation, which the two frame conditions fix injectively. Hence $B_c^\top W B_c$ is positive definite for positive diagonal $W$.

The compact integral now has ordinary isolated nondegenerate minima on the gauge slice. Points outside small invariant neighborhoods of the minimum orbits have a uniform positive action cost. Inside, the frame parametrization is smooth. The finite-dimensional Laplace argument with source derivatives from the earlier calculation applies sector by sector. The leading source-dependent term is $-\tfrac12\sum_c\log\det(B_c^\top W B_c)$. For the $1/\beta$ coefficient use independent Gaussian colors with covariances $C_c=(B_c^\top W B_c)^{-1}$, embedded by zeros at removed coordinates. Add the three density terms above to the expected fourth coefficient of the real trace and half the variance of the total cubic deficit. For two dot products use the product of the two color sums and the two same-color crossed pairings. Each scalar triple product has six signed assignments of its slots to the three colors. Grouping by the ordered triple of color-indexed edges makes its squared expectation a sum of products of three covariances. Differentiating all weight factors recovers the source insertions, including their nonlinear terms. **How the signed rational is recovered.** If $D$ is a common denominator for the three unit-source covariances, inverse derivatives for source subset $m$ have denominator dividing $D^{1+|m|}$. The three radial, quartic and cubic terms consequently have common third-derivative denominator $24D^6$. Here $D=2162160$ in the producing tree. For each subset use the largest absolute entry of its scaled covariance and bound each triple-source weight by its total absolute value over edge triples. Subset convolution of these nonnegative bounds majorizes every Wick product. The quartic bound uses the sum of absolute slot coefficients times 15 for color pairings. The cubic bound uses the product of the two total triple-weight bounds and three covariance bounds. The signed Haar and three-circle terms have direct trace and variance bounds. Their product exceeds twice the stored absolute integer bound, giving a unique signed numerator. A separate modular run in the other gauge frame uses 193. **Character control of the gauge measure.** On a two-dimensional torus with one central twist, Schur orthogonality gives
$$
 Z=\sum_{n\ge1}(-1)^{n-1}\prod_p 2e^{-x_p}I_n(x_p)/x_p,\qquad x_p=\beta w_p.
$$
The dimension factor on a torus is one, and the center evaluates to $(-1)^{n-1}$ in the $n$-dimensional representation. The sum is half the zero-index product minus half the alternating sum over all integer indices. The latter is the convolution of the circle Fourier weights at total angle $\pi$. At fixed face count and positive weights its action has strictly positive minimum under that constraint, so it is exponentially small relative to the zero-index product. In particular $A(w)=\sum_p 1/(8w_p)$ at first order. For one face, $Z=e^{-\beta}(I_0(\beta)-e^{-\beta})/\beta$. Direct normalized Haar quadrature in the two winding angles and their relative axis cosine matches that expression. The complete twisted evaluator reproduces the first coefficient and its mixed derivatives with overlapping signed sources on three two-dimensional tori in both gauge frames. This checks the frame density on a compact non-Abelian integral independent of the four-dimensional covariance computation.
