On every finite periodic cubic spatial lattice of side at least three, the actual positive vacuum of the spatial Wilson Hamiltonian requires a growing number of electric states on a link. For a compact connected simple group of dimension $d$ and magnetic/kinetic ratio $g_G$ in the metric convention below, the least rank capturing any fixed fraction of the link state is of order $g_G^{d/4}$. Its von Neumann entropy is $(d/4)\log g_G+O(1)$. Both estimates are uniform in box size. The same proof gives joint estimates for specified forests. [Dimension and entropy proof](dimension.md)

For the defining $SU(2)$ action the shell label $n=2j$, divided by $g^{1/4}$ in A4's convention, has subsequential probability densities on the half-line. A sharp low-shell inequality controls the probability loss at the origin. Central electric flux also has an explicit joint limit on **any fixed marked set**. For the six edges at one vertex, its signs converge to the uniform measure on the 32 even-parity patterns. The parity constraint holds exactly at finite coupling. On times $\tau/\lambda$ the limiting star dynamics flips each of twelve admissible edge pairs at rate one. On fixed positive physical times the connected weight of the whole bounded local center-flux algebra vanishes along bare refinement. On the longer time scale set by the spatial mesh $b$, a different family retains an absolute positive signal. Average one-link translations over $SU(2)$ conjugacy classes of angles $s_i g^{-1/4}$ for any fixed distinct $s_i>0$.
