# Verification of the magnetic band count

**Formal result.** In [BandRank.lean](BandRank.lean), `certifiedBand` takes a positive measure, a real-valued transfer variable with integrable powers, and the seven numerical moment inequalities. For any two proposed transfer values it proves **positive remaining measure** strictly inside $(1/1024,1/2)$ after removing both. It proves this for the full interval and tolerance used in the text. The inputs involve the positive-time-weighted measure; they may be supplied by any suitable transfer observable.

The proof integrates the quadratic window polynomial against every monic quadratic squared, derives its matrix entries from the seven moments, and controls the error of the resulting form. `exactMinors` evaluates the three rational minors, `sylvesterFloor` proves the fraction-free square decomposition and `momentFloor` combines them. `thirdMass` excludes a proposed two-level band even when sample points outside those levels belong only to a null set. The uniform compact-profile Fourier estimate and the use of C4’s Wilson-to-Maxwell convergence are analytic, as are continuity of the limiting density and the arbitrary-band density-of-levels deduction. [Mathematical statement](README.md)

**Recursive execution.** Each of two fresh hosted PCP desks used a root, its generation worker and two grandchildren. The computational grandchild checked the moment tolerance with exact rational arithmetic, normalized a smooth radial compact profile and evaluated its time ratios with two independent quadrature orders. It also checked the SHA-256 of C4’s deposited magnetic module, compiled it with the pinned host Lean and read all 21 axiom reports. The native grandchild booted the measured kernel, computed the quadratic-window coefficients from the dyadic endpoint exponents and emitted the Lean definition. A second native boot assembled that definition and the generated rounding-margin declaration into the Lean module. The root compared source bytes, checked its 16 axiom reports, and ran four isolated adverse modifications. The host executed Lean; the native kernels generated the designated regions and assembled the source. [Launcher](replay.py) · [Controls](controls.py) · [Native generator](certificate.c) · [Post-exit read-back](readback.txt)

The adverse variants change the sign of the localizer’s constant term, reduce the three-direction Cauchy–Schwarz coefficient to two, double the certified matrix floor, and increase the allowed data error by ten. Each is checked through the modified declaration, so an unrelated error later in a dependent theorem cannot count as the intended rejection. A separate exact three-atom fixture has two distinct in-band energies and a third outside. Its monic annihilator gives the negative localizing quadratic $-405743/41943040$.

**Numerical checks.** For the universal moments the numerical least eigenvalue is $1.5953\times10^{-8}$; the rigorous floor $1/70000000$ is smaller. The exact worst-case entry-error calculation leaves $3469/1146880000000$ of positive margin. On the unit-radius profile proportional to $e^{-1/(1-|x|^2)}$ in the open unit ball, the largest absolute ratio error at $T=16000$ was $5.12\times10^{-11}$ or smaller. The profile-independent analytic upper bound at that time is $6.104\times10^{-10}$. These are Maxwell-profile controls. No finite-cutoff four-dimensional Wilson correlator was numerically measured in this replay. The eventual finite-box conclusion follows from the stated convergence and the strict margin.
