# The spatial root-path contribution to the Haar identity

Work in a local weak limit of even isotropic Wilson tori, with $L\to\infty$ at fixed $\beta\ge2$, normalized original-link Haar measure and action $\beta\sum_p(1-\operatorname{Re}U_p)$. Here $\operatorname{Re}U_p$ is half the fundamental SU(2) trace in unit-quaternion conventions. C4’s pressure estimate gives $\sup_p\mathbb E_\mu[\beta(1-\operatorname{Re}U_p)]\le C$ independent of the periodic-limit state and coupling. For example, a temporal forest gives $V^{-1}\log[Z_L(\beta/2)/Z_L(\beta)]\le C+6\log(\beta)/L$. Jensen and isotropy bound three times the mean scaled action of a face by that pressure difference. All finite-support Ward identities are passed to each chosen state at fixed parameters through bounded continuous cylinders. A state specified only on gauge-invariant cylinders has an original-link subsequential lift by compactness. The final readings are gauge invariant and all bounds are uniform over the lifts. [C4’s periodic pressure calculation](../../central-quotients/tangent-curvature/periodic-limit/pressure.md) · [Wilson stability and multi-reflection estimates for $U(N)$, Faria da Veiga and O’Carroll](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.07376)

Use a time-first path from the origin to $z=(t,\mathbf z)$, followed by ordered signed spatial segments in directions 1, 2, 3. Let $R_z$ denote its transporter, $U'_e=R_x U_e R_{x+\hat\imath}^{-1}$ the rooted positive edge, and $F_p=\sqrt\beta\,\operatorname{Im}(R_z U_p R_z^{-1})$ the color vector of a rooted positively oriented face. Put $w(x)=1+|\mathbf x|_1$. The C4 bounds needed here are $\|F_p\|_2\le C$, $\|1-U'_e\|_2\le Cw(x)/\sqrt\beta$ and
$$
 |\mathbb E[F(d^*k)e^{i\varphi}]|\le \mathbb E|F(d^*k)|
       \le C\|k\|_{1,w}/\sqrt\beta
$$
for real finite three-cochains $k$ and any real phase $\varphi$. The rooted edge estimate commutes the last step of the path through at most $|\mathbf x|_1$ later spatial steps, including for a time edge. For closedness, telescope the six monotone paths through a cube. Their consecutive ratios are its six signed face words, conjugated by prefixes of at most one rooted link. Each ratio minus one has $L^2$ norm $O(\beta^{-1/2})$. The nonlinear part of the finite product has expected norm $O(\beta^{-1})$ by Cauchy–Schwarz and boundedness of the factors. Removing the short conjugations costs $O(w/\sqrt\beta)$ after scaling. Summing against $k$ gives the bound above. [C4’s cube and root estimates](../../central-quotients/tangent-curvature/periodic-limit/rate/ward.md)

For real colored cochains, $\|\cdot\|_1$ sums Euclidean color norms over oriented cells, $\|\cdot\|_{1,w}$ includes the spatial weight, and $\|\cdot\|_\infty$ is the supremum of those color norms. Suppose $h$ is a finite one-cochain supported on **spatial** edges and $v$ is a finite two-cochain of any orientation. Then
$$
 \left|\mathbb E[F(dh)e^{iF(v)}]
          -i\langle dh,v\rangle\,\mathbb E e^{iF(v)}\right|
 \le \frac{C}{\sqrt\beta}
  \left[\|h\|_{1,w}(1+\|v\|_\infty)+\|h\|_\infty\|v\|_{1,w}\right]. \tag{1}
$$

For a single positive edge $e$ based at $x$, use a unit imaginary direction $X$. If the ordered root path to $x$ traverses $e$, replace that traversal by the other three sides of the adjacent square. The new path $\widetilde R_x$ avoids $e$ in both orientations and differs in $L^2$ by $O(\beta^{-1/2})$. With other links held fixed the generator $\widetilde R_x^{-1}X\widetilde R_x$ is a constant of the integration over $U_e$. Vary $U_e$ by its left exponential at scale $1/\sqrt\beta$. For a face word $A U_e B$ or $A U_e^{-1} B$, take the prefix $V=A$ or $A U_e^{-1}$ and incidence $\epsilon=+1$ or $-1$, respectively. The generator in the directly varied rooted face is $\epsilon\,\operatorname{Ad}(R_zV\widetilde R_x^{-1})X$. Factor $R_z V\widetilde R_x^{-1}=(R_z V R_x^{-1})(R_x\widetilde R_x^{-1})$. The first factor is a product of at most four primed links. The second is a conjugate of one detour plaquette or the identity. Thus the conjugated signed generator differs from the signed $X$ by $O(w(x)/\sqrt\beta)$ in $L^2$. The negative log-density derivative is the sum of the signed generator dotted with $F_p$ over incident faces, since $\operatorname{Re}(Xq)=-X\cdot\operatorname{Im}q$. Its $L^1$ error against the unit-edge $F(dh)$ is $O(w(x)/\sqrt\beta)$.

The direct contribution to differentiating the phase has expected error at most $Cw(x)\sum_{p\ni e}|v_p|/\sqrt\beta$, by the same generator comparison and the unscaled face estimate. There is also a contribution for each face whose root path contains $e$, whether or not it is incident on $e$. If the path is $A U_e^{\pm1} B$, its logarithmic derivative $\dot R_z R_z^{-1}$ has norm $1/\sqrt\beta$; the prefix for an inverse includes $U_e^{-1}$. The root contribution to the scaled face derivative is the commutator of that logarithmic derivative with its scaled imaginary part. Its norm is at most $2|F_p|/\sqrt\beta$. This estimate holds for arbitrary colors. In particular the commutator must be retained even when two Fourier directions are different. Multiply the edge identity by $|h_e|$ and sum, taking its direction to be $h_e/|h_e|$ when nonzero. Each edge is incident on six faces. Therefore the direct errors, including the action term, cost at most $C\|h\|_{1,w}(1+\|v\|_\infty)/\sqrt\beta$. For the path term interchange the finite sums. For a face based at $z$, its root path makes exactly $|\mathbf z|_1$ spatial steps. Its temporal segment contributes zero because $h$ has no temporal component. Thus
$$
 \sum_p |v_p|\sum_{e\in R_z}|h_e|\le\|h\|_\infty\sum_p|\mathbf z|_1|v_p|.
$$
This proves (1) with no factor of the temporal separation. The Haar variation, generator scale and subsequent absolute-value estimate apply to the entire insertion at each fixed finite support. The underlying Wilson relation is the lattice relation in the original link coordinates.

Let $u=dh$. Apply (1) to $v=\theta u$, $0\le\theta\le1$. The function $\phi(\theta)=\mathbb E e^{i\theta F(u)}$ satisfies $\phi'+\theta\|u\|_2^2\phi=\mathrm{err}(\theta)$ with error bounded by the right side of (1) at $v=u$. Integrating from $\phi(0)=1$ multiplies the inhomogeneity by a factor of modulus at most one. This gives the same bound for $|\mathbb E e^{iF(u)}-e^{-\|u\|_2^2/2}|$. The norm in this exponent is the ordinary square-summable cochain norm.
