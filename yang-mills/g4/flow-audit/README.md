## The sign at lattice flow time 20

Use the microscopic Gaussian curvature limit in C4's fixed-time argument. On the four-dimensional Brillouin zone let \(a(t)=2-2\cos t,\ A=\sum_{j=1}^4 a(k_j)\) and \(Q=(a(k_1)+a(k_2))/A\). The value at \(A=0\) is immaterial. For a spatial \(12\)-plaquette, normalize \(Qe^{-sA}\,dk\) to a probability law, with \(s=2\tau=40\). Write \(T=k_4\) under that law and \(m_j=\mathbb ET^j\). The normalized linear covariance at separation \(n\) is \(\mathbb E\cos(nT)\). Squaring it gives the normalized connected covariance of the quadratic plaquette reading by Wick contraction. The common factor from the three colors cancels. If \(T'\) is an independent copy, symmetry makes this square \(\mathbb E\cos(n(T+T'))\).

Set \(u=T+T'\). After division by the positive equal-time connected variance the reflected form of the slice-1 minus slice-2 reading is \(\mathbb E D(u)\), where
\[
 D(u)=\cos(2u)-2\cos(3u)+\cos(4u)=-2\cos(3u)(1-\cos u).
\]
For real \(u\),
\[
 D(u)\le -u^2+\tfrac{55}{12}u^4 .
\]
Indeed \(\cos(3u)\ge1-9u^2/2\), while \(u^2/2-u^4/24\le1-\cos u\le u^2/2\). Independence, symmetry and Cauchy–Schwarz give \(\mathbb Eu^2=2m_2\) and \(\mathbb Eu^4=2m_4+6m_2^2\le8m_4\).

### Bounding the temporal frequencies

The time marginal density is proportional to \(e^{-s a(t)}h(a(t))\). Integrate out the spatial modes to obtain
\[
 h(y)=\int_{[-\pi,\pi]^3}e^{-sB}\frac{a(k_1)+a(k_2)}{B+y}\,d^3 k,
 \qquad B=\sum_{j=1}^3a(k_j).
\]
It is finite, positive and decreasing on \(y\ge0\). In the measure proportional to \(t^2e^{-s a(t)}dt\), the functions \(t^2\) and \(h(a(t))\) are oppositely ordered in \(|t|\). Their covariance is nonpositive, as seen by multiplying their differences on two independent samples. Dividing by the positive mean of \(h\) gives \(m_4/m_2\le I_4/I_2\), where \(I_j=\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}t^j e^{-s a(t)}dt\). This removes the spatial projection factor without a small-momentum approximation.

On \(|t|\le1\), \(11t^2/12\le a(t)\le t^2\); on \(1\le|t|\le\pi\), \(a(t)\ge11/12\). Gaussian integration and an exterior tail bound give
\[
 I_2\ge\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2s^{3/2}}(1-2^{3/2}e^{-s/2}),\qquad
 I_4\le\frac{3\sqrt\pi}{4s^{5/2}}(12/11)^{5/2}+2\pi^5e^{-11s/12}.
\]
For the first inequality subtract the tail \(|t|>1\) from the full Gaussian second moment, bounding that tail by \(e^{-s/2}\int_{\mathbb R}t^2e^{-s t^2/2}dt\). At \(s=40\), use \((12/11)^{5/2}<5/4,\ \pi<4,\ 40^{3/2}<256,\ e>2\). The resulting rational upper bound on \(I_4/I_2\) is \(49160/1048573<1/20\). The marginal has positive variance. Hence
\[

\]
All frequencies and lags in this argument are in lattice units. The fixed-time convergence of joint action moments transfers this strict inequality to every sufficiently large even \(\ell=\beta\).
