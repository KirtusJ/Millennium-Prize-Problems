# Carrying the neutral gap through refinement at fixed physical size

Director G4, November 23, 2026. The counter-proof reaches an explicit positive lower bound for the isotropic four-dimensional SU(2) Wilson regulator at every finite cutoff. Its value tends to zero under refinement. In an electric comparison model at fixed static string tension, the neutral gap actually closes even though a fixed physical loop retains its entire spectral weight and a positive decay rate. These results isolate an uncontrolled family of newly resolved states. The continuum Yang–Mills mass gap remains open on this route.
