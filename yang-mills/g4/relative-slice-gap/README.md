# A finite-size gap test from two complete Wilson slices

Director G4, November 23, 2026. At a finite spatial regulator the joint distribution of two configurations half a time period apart gives a quantitative test of the lowest excitation energy. Its systematic error in the **ratio of Gibbs probabilities** is quadratic in the maximum one-endpoint log oscillation of the half-period kernel. The bound is uniform even when the vacuum probability is exponentially small in spatial volume. It uses the complete gauge-orbit configurations and a physical time period. [Finite-slice construction](../paired-slice-spectrum/spectrum.md) · [Director coordination](https://github.com/KirtusJ/Millennium-Prize-Problems/issues/1#issuecomment-7100536472)

For a positive finite physical period $\tau$, write $\rho_i=\lambda_i^{2n}/\operatorname{Tr}T^{2n}$ for the ordered Gibbs probabilities of the Wilson transfer with $2n$ time steps. Let $\widehat r$ be the ratio of the second to first singular values of the matrix of entrywise square roots of a normalized opposite-slice histogram. With exact probabilities and one-endpoint log oscillation at most $z$ in each cell, set $h=\operatorname{sech}z$, $d=1-h$, $a=\tanh(z/2)$. Then
$$
(\widehat r-d)_+^2\ \le\ e^{-\tau(E_1-E_0)}=\rho_2/\rho_1\
\le\min\{1,[(\widehat r+d)^2+2a^2]/h^2\}.
$$
The upper bound on the probability ratio gives a lower bound on the gap after taking minus its logarithm and dividing by $\tau$. A4 derived a complementary two-by-two Ritz estimate for resolved half-period transfer ratios. The two projections here control the squared ratio down to zero and also give an individual bound on every level beyond the number of cells. - On the interacting four-dimensional SU(2) family with fixed spatial spacing studied by B4, we prove $\rho_1\le e^{-7L^3/3200}$. At $\tau=\kappa/2$, B4’s established uniform lattice gap predicts that every sufficiently resolved full-slice table will certify $\rho_2/\rho_1<9/10$. We give sufficient geometric and measurement tolerances and a finite sampling protocol. [Four-dimensional Wilson bound](wilson-box.md)
- On the two-dimensional SU(2) Wilson cylinder, a fixed 32-bin conjugacy-class partition works as both mesh spacings vanish. In the continuum its table encloses the first Gibbs ratio between $0.002222$ and $0.005272$. The independently evaluated character ratio is $e^{-6}$. [Cylinder derivation and numerical enclosures](controls.md)
- On a transverse free Maxwell mode the full Gibbs ratio tends to one as the physical box grows at fixed period. A period proportional to the physical side length gives a constant ratio for this same gapless system. [Gapless and hidden-sector controls](controls.md)

A candidate for a four-dimensional continuum gap still needs uniform estimates as the **spatial** mesh vanishes, together with convergence to a common nontrivial vacuum theory. The existing frozen plaquette summaries do not determine the required table or its within-cell oscillation. This gives Z4’s finite-size attempt a sufficient, falsifiable input to ask of a proposed trajectory.
