# Fixed even-shift blocks

Write \(V^{(i)}_{jk}(x)\) for the covariance of the two transverse face pairs obtained by integrating the link at \(x\) with all other links fixed. Evaluate this one-link formula at **every** link of direction \(i\). On a selected link it also gives the covariance conditional on the full mask exterior. Off the mask its role is to define a stationary extension, and it may depend on selected links. Pull the stationary field on the spatial torus back periodically to the lattice when using a common index group along the sequence. Center each entry by its own cutoff mean and call it \(X_n(x)\). The Hilbert space \(L^2(\mathbb P_n)\) may change with \(n\).

For a particular pair of smooth orientation profiles the coefficient is a finitely supported signed weight \(w_n(x)=b^4\mathbf1_{M_i}(x) f_{ij}(x_e)g_{ik}(x_e)\), with the obvious specialization to a variance. Its absolute sum is bounded. The conditional face-pair Cauchy–Schwarz and Jensen inequalities with the uniform action moments give a uniform \(L^2\) bound \(B\) for \(X_n(x)\) and uniform integrability for products at fixed offsets. Sums over the finitely many indices cover the six orientations by polarization and the triangle inequality.

Let \(T\) be a finite set of admissible shifts and \(a_u\ge0\) sum to one. Translating the field to \(X_n(x+u)\) pushes the deterministic weights to \(\tau_u w_n(x)=w_n(x-u)\). Adding and subtracting the weighted reading of \(\overline X_{n,T}(x)=\sum_{u\in T}a_u X_n(x+u)\) yields

\[
 \left\|\sum_x w_n(x) X_n(x)\right\|_2
 \le B\max_{u\in T}\|w_n-\tau_u w_n\|_1
      + \|w_n\|_1\sup_x\|\overline X_{n,T}(x)\|_2.
\]

For fixed even shifts in all four axes, transverse parity is preserved. Smoothness costs \(O_{f,g}(b|u|)\). Slab changes and omitted edge layers cost \(O_{f,g}(|u_1|/m_b+b|u|)\) on a fixed compact support. Count only the \(O(|u_1|)\) layers per slab boundary that a translate can cross. The number of slab boundaries on a compact support is \(O(1+(bm_b)^{-1})\). The periodic cuts and incomplete far slab eventually miss the compact set and its fixed translates. Stationarity of the extended link statistic makes the block norm independent of its base point. Odd shifts can give a nonvanishing weight defect even for a perfectly periodic mask.

Take \(T_K=2\{0,\ldots,K-1\}^4\) with equal weights. At fixed \(K\) all pairs of stars lie at fixed offsets in the lift. Joint finite-patch tangent convergence and uniform integrability give convergence of every centered covariance product. One-star marginal convergence alone would permit a common random entry repeated at every site. The limiting radial term is constant and disappears upon centering. For each local centered Gram entry the limit is a finite linear combination of centered products of Gaussian linear curvature variables. The local differences and transverse projections are finite stencils. Each two-point cross covariance of the linear variables has a bounded multiplier on the finite Brillouin torus, so its Fourier coefficients are square summable. Wick expresses the covariance of two centered products as two products of such coefficients. Cauchy–Schwarz makes each product absolutely summable on the full lattice, hence also on the even sublattice.

If \(S\) bounds the sum of absolute covariances on the even sublattice, each covariance-matrix row on \(T_K\) has absolute row sum at most \(S\). There are \(K^4\) rows. The equal-weight variance of the limiting block is at most \(S/K^4\). Thus the block norm in the weighted comparison is at most \(\sqrt S/K^2\) after the cutoff limit. First choose a fixed block for the desired accuracy, then remove the cutoff. A rate for \(K=K(n)\) would require uniform control of increasing-patch errors.

Its eigenvalues are \(1/8,(1+2d)/24,d/8\) with multiplicities \(1,3,2\). In particular the contact contribution has full rank. **Conditioning on an infinite mask.** For a finite subset of selected links, condition first on its complement. With no plaquette containing two selected links, the finite-link Gibbs density factors, and all its staples depend only on the exterior of the full mask. Conditioning back down to that exterior preserves this product for each finite subset. In the fixed-box infinite-time vacuum the finite-link specification follows from finite periodic histories for continuous cylinder tests. The normalized finite-link integral against fixed external links is a continuous local function, since the compact integral has continuous density and a strictly positive normalizer. Pass the cylinder identity to the vacuum limit, then extend by a monotone class. This step uses the original-link vacuum cylinder law. It also explains why the same exterior can be used for all compactly supported tests.
