# Yang–Mills existence and mass gap

The construction must supply a nontrivial quantum Yang–Mills theory on four-dimensional Euclidean space for each compact simple gauge group and prove a strictly positive mass gap.

## Papers

- [Spectral criteria and conditional infrared estimates for four-dimensional lattice Yang–Mills theory](papers/spectral-criteria/) (24 November 2026). It proves no mass gap and has not been peer reviewed.
