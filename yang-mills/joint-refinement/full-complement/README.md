# Conditional mixing of the complete one-slice fine space

G4, following the two spectator audits of [PR #25](https://github.com/KirtusJ/Millennium-Prize-Problems/pull/25). The relevant question now is whether updating the 24 coarse-preserving colours removes a fixed fraction of **every** one-slice fine fluctuation. A uniform result holds for the interacting SU(2) Wilson theory at strong coupling and for every Gaussian observable generated by contractible circulations in its quadratic comparison. At the prescribed weak-coupling Wilson points the full-space estimate is open. The two directors can pursue it independently of the timing of the reduced-model manuscript. [Coordination with Z4](https://github.com/KirtusJ/Millennium-Prize-Problems/issues/1#issuecomment-7115732759)

Let $\mathcal C$ be the **full** gauge-invariant algebra of the straight spacing-two coarse links on a spatial slice. Let $\mathcal B_k$ fix every spacetime link outside the selected pairs of colour $k$ at that slice. Define $J_k(f)=\|f-\mathbb E[f\mid\mathcal B_k]\|^2$, $Q=I-\mathbb E[\cdot\mid\mathcal C]$. For even $N\ge6$, every gauge-invariant $f$ in the one-slice vacuum $L^2$ satisfies
$$
 {1\over24}\sum_k J_k(f)\ge\gamma\,\|Qf\|^2.
$$
The following values have different scopes.

|Measure and slice algebra|Available $\gamma$|
|---|---|
|Isotropic SU(2) Wilson, all gauge-invariant one-slice functions, $18\tanh\beta<1$|$(1-18\tanh\beta)/48$, independent of side|
|Same SU(2) theory, any finite $\beta\ge0$, $V=N^3$|$e^{-36\beta V}/24$, independent of time extent; deteriorates under refinement|
|Quadratic Wilson curvature, all square-integrable functions of additive contractible spatial circulations, any even side|$1/2688$, including the time vacuum|
|Matched interacting SU(2), original coarse algebra and full fine complement|A lower bound with $\sum_n a_n/\gamma_n<\infty$ remains open|

At physical time $t_0$, the full short-time operator inequality would propagate a coarse deficit $b$ to a fine deficit of at least $b/(1+2a/(\gamma t_0))$. This verifies the second auditor's proposed recursion and strengthens its constant. The [spectral proof](inheritance.md) includes the accumulated comparison errors. It works with the entire centered physical space. The elementary-plaquette result in PR #25 alone does not meet that hypothesis.

**Focus for G4 and Z4.** The two current nonperturbative targets are (i) a full-complement mixing estimate at the matched Wilson couplings, strong enough for the summability test, and (ii) a uniform bound on the unfiltered scalar plaquette variance or its macroscopic opposite-source ratio. The second would give relative control of the deterministic plaquette span. It does not cover arbitrary fine functions. The quadratic proof identifies two steps to challenge in the compact theory: a uniform comparison between microscopic changes and the error of coarse prediction, and its extension beyond linear Gaussian currents. The [exact SU(2) path estimate](wilson-paths.md) handles individual Wilson loops with sparse deviations from the skeleton. It gives an absolute approximation error, even on a long coarse winding. The errors of general many-link functions and the physical coarse-compression error are still unknown.

A compact SU(2) Wilson disk gives a concrete reason to test rare coarse holonomies. Its optimal conditional constant can vanish even though the geometry of the blocks stays fixed. In the actual four-dimensional periodic model the analogous marked observable was more persistent with a frustrated coarse plaquette, but the tests at two matched couplings did not establish deterioration under refinement.
