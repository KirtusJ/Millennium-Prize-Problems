# Targeting a specified coarse orbit in four-dimensional SU(2)

The compact disk suggests a test that the unweighted replica streams cannot resolve: a face with coarse holonomy $-I$ and an independent colour reference. We tested a corresponding raw skeleton directly on periodic four-dimensional lattices. At side six and its matched bare coupling the four-face mark's estimated loss-to-variance ratio fell from $0.122(5)$ with boundary $+I$ to $0.0464(55)$ with boundary $-I$. At side eight the latter ratio was $0.0441(20)$. This identifies a more discriminating trial function. These finite-torus estimates do not establish vanishing of the ratio with refinement.

**Conditioning and source.** Freeze *every* straight skeleton edge on time slice zero. Take all to be the identity, except for the positive $y$ edge at $(2,0,0)$ set to $\pm I$ and the positive $z$ edge there set to the imaginary quaternion $i$. The coarse $xy$ square based at zero then has holonomy $W=\pm I$, and the coarse $xz$ square at zero has holonomy $B=i$. Other coarse squares sharing the changed edges are affected. The reference is part of the four-dimensional Wilson action; the disk's independent colour average need not survive.

On the marked square take its four fine $xy$ plaquettes and transport each to the root along a path of positive $x$ steps followed by positive $y$ steps. Call these quaternions $g_j$, and put $\bar g=\sum_jg_j/4$. The two real tests are $\Re(Bg_1)$ and $\Re(B\bar g)$, gauge invariant before fixing any skeleton. The spread $1-|\bar g|^2$ measures disagreement among the four face matrices. At a fixed raw skeleton the coarse predictor is a constant. Estimate its full conditional variance by the pooled centered second moment. Estimate the numerator by the exact two-copy refresh of each of the 24 colours, averaging the squared differences as in the [unweighted pilot](replicas.md). The same gauge-orbit argument gives these quantities conditional on this coarse orbit. Strictly positive finite-volume densities make the integrals continuous in the frozen edges. An **exact** small ratio on one specified skeleton would therefore upper-bound the best global constant by taking coarse gates in its neighbourhood. Monte Carlo errors and possible preparation bias enter the reported ratios.

An independent side-eight repeat used both signs, 864 discarded sweeps and 256 samples per stream. The four-face ratios were $0.1143\pm0.0114$ at $+I$ and $0.0506\pm0.0013$ at $-I$. Cold-start and hot-start estimates on the negative background were 0.0495 and 0.0518, with respective estimated variances 0.0595 and 0.0573. This repeat sustains the contrast between signs within the finite periodic preparation. It changes the seeds and run lengths, so it does not isolate burn-in bias. The mean spreads were 0.3361 and 0.3341, respectively. The detectable change is in the variance and persistence of the relative-colour mark, rather than a resolved change in the mean spread.

The high-$\beta$ run initially used five-staple proposals accepted against the shared plaquette, which stalled on some twisted pairs. The partial runs are retained in the evidence. The completed run uses a recentered exact proposal. With shared half-trace $u^TOv$ and one-link staples $h_u,h_v$, choose any $\alpha$, propose the links independently with staples $h_u-\alpha,h_v+O^T\alpha$, then accept with probability
$$\exp[-\tfrac\beta2|u-Ov-\alpha|^2].$$
Expansion of the square recovers the target pair weight up to a constant independent of the proposed variables. The program takes $\alpha=u_{\rm old}-Ov_{\rm old}$ once per draw; the accepted law is identical for every starting pair. It obtains $Ov$ and $O^T\alpha$ by multilinearity of the common-face staple. No change to the conditional Wilson law is required. [Rejection sampler](replicas.rs)

For G4 and Z4 the open estimate is the smallest conditional loss over **all** coarse orbits and slice tests, allowing rates compatible with the physical-time sum. The data distinguish an actual coarse-background effect from an extrapolation of the disk. Testing two spatially separated frustrated coarse faces with a relative colour observable would further distinguish alignment by the surrounding field from loss of a local colour direction.
