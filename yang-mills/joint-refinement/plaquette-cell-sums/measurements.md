# Four-dimensional covariance on one fully clear orbit

G4 measured the collective mark on a single prescribed orbit at each of the two matched couplings. Every straight skeleton link is the identity, giving distance $\pi$ from $-I$ for every coarse plaquette. The reference coarse loop is the identity as well, so taking the half-trace removes all path transports. The one-cell mark is the average of the four $xy$ half-traces inside a coarse square. We include every $xy$ cell at even $z$ on slice zero. The resulting 27 or 64 components sample the full translation family of this mark. Z4's runs use a traceless reference to probe a transverse direction; those measurements concern a different observable and orbit.

The zero mode uses $1/\sqrt M$ normalization. The zero-mode variance divided by one-cell variance was $.993\pm.078$ and $1.047\pm.078$ on sides six and eight. No large enhancement is resolved at these two sizes on this orbit. The coherent measurement forms the Fourier transform of the signed difference of the two full redraws before squaring. The diagonal prediction pools cell-wise squared differences; their equality is an expectation identity, and agreement here is a check of the instrument. The table's $J/V$ uses the diagonal prediction.

The smallest fitted quotient across momenta was .08084(.00074) on six and .08121(.00192) on eight. These in-sample minima can have selection bias. With two streams selecting the momentum and the other two estimating the quotient, the two reciprocal splits gave .08150 and .10206 on six, .09490 and .08529 on eight. At zero momentum cold- and hot-start estimates were .09349 and .08164 on six, .08430 and .08346 on eight. The finite time preparation, small stream count and single clear orbit limit inference.

**Haar control.** On the fixed identity skeleton the four spokes are independent Haar at $\beta=0$. A fine plaquette's half-trace has variance $1/4$. Two different elementary plaquettes in the same cell have distinct spokes, at least one appearing in only one of them; integrating that spoke gives zero covariance. One cell therefore has variance $1/16$. For each of the four active colours the refreshed pair removes three of the four half-traces under Haar conditional expectation and leaves the fourth unchanged. Its expected conditional loss is $3/64$, contributing to the average over all 24 colours. Thus $J=1/128$ and $J/V=1/8$ on every mode. The remaining twenty colours cannot change any of these marks, which the Rust instrument verifies per draw at the first sample of each stream. [Exact diagonal identity](diagonal.md)

**Reproduction.** The program uses the original Wilson conditional sweeps, pair heat baths, colour indices and square-loop calculation from [the fixed-orbit source](../full-complement/replicas.rs). The [build script](build.py) inserts the [multi-cell entry point](cells.rs) next to those routines. Run it with the path to rustc and the desired binary output path. The binary takes bare coupling, seed, hot flag, fine side, burn count and sample count. At each point there are four streams with seeds $b+7919i$ and alternating cold/hot starts. Base seeds are 3320013, 3420013 and 3520013 for Haar, six and eight. Each interacting run discards 384 conditional sweeps, the Haar run discards eight; all use 160 observations four sweeps apart. The time circumference is $2N$. At each observation the measurement draws two copies of every active colour at the same exterior and restores the original links between colours. Separate random streams drive the simulation and measurement. The reported errors delete one full stream at a time. [Raw signed redraw differences](cells.zip) · [Reduction](cells.py)
