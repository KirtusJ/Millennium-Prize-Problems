### The same two operators

For the three winding holonomies $P_i=(p_i,\mathbf v_i)$ set $s_i=|\mathbf v_i|^2=1-p_i^2$ and $A_i=(4p_i^2-1)/3=1-4s_i/3$. Z4’s first $E$ probe uses the orthonormal components $(s_1-s_2)/\sqrt2$ and $(s_1+s_2-2s_3)/\sqrt6$. G4 uses all three cyclic $A_j-A_k$. For triples $s,s'$ the sum of products of cyclic differences is $3\langle P s,P s'\rangle$, where $P$ is projection onto the traceless plane. Averaging over three G4 components and two Z4 components gives $C_{E,\rm G4}=(32/9)C_{E,\rm Z4}$. The first $T_2$ probes are both $p_jp_k\,\mathbf v_j\cdot\mathbf v_k$ up to ordering. The relations commute with spatial averaging, expectation, and one-point subtraction. Each normalized single-operator ratio $C(t)/C(0)$ is identical provided the fields, ensemble, boundary preparation and times agree.

The test sends fourth roots of 193 triples into G4’s Rust sampler as constant spatial link fields on a four-site-side box, measures the resulting winding holonomies, and calls Z4’s deposited Python operator on the targets. It checks the three tensor components and the $E$ covariance identity on all pairs of triples. Z4’s published energies use a four-operator variational analysis, and G4’s fixed-time slopes use a single reading. The identity links their inputs without identifying those estimators. ### The shared open estimate

The normalized transfer at physical time $\ell$ is $S_N=(T_N/\lambda_{0,N})^N$. Z4’s reduction requires a positive gap in the volume limit at the required bare couplings; G4’s fixed-scale approach requires a contraction on the whole neutral vacuum complement, including new states under subdivision. A fixed-box asymptotic with an $N$-dependent remainder cannot be substituted along the matched sequence. The presently measured winding rates also leave states orthogonal to the probes uncontrolled. Our common test for future claims is to specify the vacuum and clock, show a uniform estimate on the new fine states and their mixing, and take the physical-volume limit separately. Exact matched electric controls exhibit both a surviving observable and a closing neutral gap.
