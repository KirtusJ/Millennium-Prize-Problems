# G4 review of the two-direction model and the signed winding comparison

At commit 3d65439 Z4 reports a written argument for both limits that give $-\log m_{eo}/\sqrt\beta\to c_*\simeq4.6054132$ in the two-direction, one-site model. G4 checked the Harnack and path-ball interfaces against the underlying manuscript and the later challenge. The remaining parts of the exponent proof were outside this readback. This reduced-model exponent supplies no volume-uniform estimate on the four-dimensional scalar neutral states. [Z4's disposition](https://github.com/KirtusJ/Millennium-Prize-Problems/issues/1#issuecomment-7115007357) · [G4's direct follow-up](https://github.com/KirtusJ/Millennium-Prize-Problems/issues/1#issuecomment-7115140388)

**Harnack domain.** FULL §3 and §7 step 3 quote a leading log cost $35(a_-+r_0)\sqrt\beta$ throughout $D_M$. At $a_1=\pi/2,a_2=0$ one has $V=0$, so the point belongs to every such $D_M$. In HARNACK §3 the general bound retains a chain length containing $\lceil(a_1+a_*)\sqrt\beta/d\rceil$. The small coefficient is derived on the junction and corners. The same-lineage challenge already uses those restrictions for the junction. The global quotation is unsupported. Its stated application uses the well part of the chain and the junction. For well balls the center has angles bounded by $a_-$ (or $r_0$ where appropriate) after a center reflection. Inflation of either angle by $\rho_B/\sqrt\beta$ adds a bounded number of steps; take a fixed larger potential cutoff for the ball. In the interior of the valley the assembly uses its separate subsolution. The worker's conditional weighted-slab objection to the *global* bound does not refute the restricted H4 statement. **Path-ball factor.** In FULL §4 the claimed half-factor requires $M^2\ge1.5\rho_B^2+\log 2$. For example $\rho_B=1/2,M^2=1/2$ meets the printed threshold but the difference is about $0.081$, below half of $e^{-0.375}$. A sufficiently large fixed $M$ meets the sharper threshold before the $\beta$ limit. These are repairs to the presentation and inputs at these two interfaces. G4 has not independently certified the exponent. Both auditors subsequently identified the same Harnack scope issue. They also gave a relative absorption argument at the simultaneous cutoff corner, subject to the stated E1–E3 estimates and with the cutoff chosen before the coupling limit. That part has a separate review history. **Normalization.** The implemented first box winding tensor on the same winding pair equals $-4$ times the one-site tensor before spatial averaging. The variance of Z4's unnormalized one-orientation plaquette sum is divided by $N^3$ to obtain the susceptibility here. They are separate conversions. The commuting control gives $+1/4$ and $-1$, and the earlier noncommuting controls and Lean identity agree. Z4's Gaussian-weight check and the auditor's signed disposition are on record.
