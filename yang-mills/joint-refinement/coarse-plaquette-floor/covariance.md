# The part of the covariance removed by the two-link integration is uniformly bounded

There is a uniform **upper** bound on the part of the raw and coarse-residual covariance removed by the new conditioning. For deterministic plaquette coefficients let $G$ be their raw covariance matrix and $G_f$ their covariance after projection off the original coarse algebra. For each colour $k$ let $M_{k,q}=\mathbb E[X_q\mid\mathcal B_k]$ with $\mathcal B_k$ the one-slice pair exterior in spacetime. Define the positive matrix $J_k=\mathbb E\,\operatorname{Cov}(X\mid\mathcal B_k)$ and its colour average $\bar J$. At every even spatial side $N\ge6$ and $\beta\ge1$,
$$
 \delta I\preceq\bar J\preceq7K I,\quad
 G-\bar J=\operatorname{avg}_k\operatorname{Cov}(M_k),\quad
 G_f-\bar J=\operatorname{avg}_k\operatorname{Cov}((I-P_{\mathcal C})M_k).
$$
Here $\delta=1/31104$ and $K$ is the deposited uniform spatial single-plaquette variance bound. Every $J_k(q,r)$ vanishes if the periodic maximum-coordinate distance between the two basepoints exceeds **one**, including between different orientations. The same conclusions for a single face orientation use eight colours, $\delta=1/10368$ and upper bound $9K/8$. The new step relative to the previous checkerboard correction is that *every one of these exteriors fixes the coarse links*.

For the upper bound, a chosen pair touches at most seven measured spatial faces: four through each spatial link and one shared face counted twice. All seven contain the common odd corner, so all their basepoints differ by at most one in every coordinate. Distinct selected pairs touch disjoint sets of measured faces. Conditionally, contributions from distinct pairs are independent. For the incident set $I$ of one pair,
$$
 \mathbb E\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\sum_{q\in I}c_q X_q\mid\mathcal B_k\right)
 \le\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\sum_{q\in I}c_q X_q\right)
 \le |I|K\sum_{q\in I}|c_q|^2 .
$$
Sum within the colour to obtain $7K I$ for each $J_k$. If only $xy$ is measured and only the eight $xy$ colours used, a chosen pair touches its own face and the two neighbours across its selected edges. Conversely, any $xy$ face is touched only by its own pair and by the two neighbours across its odd-transverse-coordinate edges. Those neighbouring pairs choose the shared edge as well. Hence across eight colours its coefficient is counted exactly three times. Averaging gives $9K/8$. This counting uses the even periodic side including seams. The [two-link lower estimate](score.md) yields the lower bound for the average. The mixed derivative estimates the loss unconditionally in every coefficient direction without positivity of an off-diagonal covariance.

The matrix identities follow by total covariance and $\mathcal C\subset\mathcal B_k$. Subtracting the coarse projection is a fixed subtraction on each conditional fibre. Distant raw covariances equal those of the corresponding conditional means for each colour. Distant residual covariances equal those of the corresponding coarse-projected means as well. At time lag $d\ge2$ the same identities for the two-point functions hold with *no* innovation term, by the common exterior at both ends. A local mean for a touched face depends only on the two-link action neighbourhood, so its conditional law is unchanged when conditioning on the other endpoint's exterior under the simultaneous construction. Integrating the pairs can destroy fine translations; the covariance matrix identities do not require simultaneous fine-momentum diagonalization. The upper estimate uses deterministic coefficients. A coarse-dependent coefficient could weight atypical exteriors and requires its own weighted upper estimate.

For these one-orientation sums even the missing block tail of the integrated part is uniformly small. Origin-average its covariance and write $j(r)=V^{-1}\sum_x\bar J_{x,x+r}$. This is equivalent to averaging the cube variance over all placements. The support has $r_z=0$, $|r_x|_N,|r_y|_N\le1$, and each entry is at most $9K/8$ in absolute value by the operator norm. With $w_L(r)$ the overlap fraction, $1-w_L(r)\le(|r_x|_N+|r_y|_N)/L$. The sum of these distances over the nine offsets is 12. Thus the absolute difference between the full innovation and its origin-averaged cube variance is at most $27K/(2L)$. This is a summable dyadic tail bound in the interacting Wilson measure for the integrated part. All slower large-scale contributions to the raw block tail must come from the conditional means. No sign condition on $j(r)$ enters this estimate.

For the unfiltered sum of one orientation set $c_q=V^{-1/2}$. Its mean contribution removed in this construction is between $1/10368$ and $9K/8$, uniformly along refinement. Thus all potentially unbounded variance remains in the conditional means. If only its *residual* part matters, the same exact statement holds after subtracting the coarse prediction. An upper bound on the remaining long-range means under the prescribed interacting Wilson trajectory is the outstanding estimate.
