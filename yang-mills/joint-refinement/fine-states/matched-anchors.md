# How well can retained winding lines predict the full reading?

Measure a bare cyclic $E$ component on the open Wilson trajectories whose independent Schrödinger-functional scale is $g^2_{\rm SF}(\ell)=2.38$. Use the three even spatial sides $N=6,8,10$ and coarse sides $3,4,5$, with $\ell$ fixed. The source and sink lie at $5\ell/2$ and $7\ell/2$ in a slab of length $6\ell$. Only unsmeared straight links enter. This makes each retained winding a function of exactly the blocked holonomies used to define the coarse algebra. [Scale setting and ensemble parameters](../../g4/full-attempt/physical-refinement/methods.md) · [Francesconi et al., Tables 2–3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.13734#page=12)

Let $C_\xi$ be the winding reading averaged over sites with the specified three parity bits $\xi$ and $F=2^{-3}\sum_\xi C_\xi$ the full reading. Put $\Delta_\xi=C_\xi-F$. Spatial translation invariance gives $\langle F,C_\xi\rangle=\|F\|^2$ and $\langle F,\Delta_\xi\rangle=0$. The cubic $E$ components have zero mean at each slice by permutation symmetry. Averaging origins kills the mixed terms exactly in each configuration, also across the two times. Write $v=\mathbb E F^2$, $d=\mathbb E\Delta_\xi^2$, averaged over components and the two symmetric slices. The least-squares predictor of $F$ using a scalar multiple of $C_\xi$ has coefficient $v/(v+d)$ and fractional squared error $d/(v+d)$. Conditional expectation onto *all* of the coarse orbit algebra can only improve on this single predictor. For product Haar the error is exactly $3/4$. [Haar calculation](winding.md)

For physical-time diagnostics use $v_\ell=\mathbb E F_-F_+$ and $d_\ell=\mathbb E\Delta_{\xi,-}\Delta_{\xi,+}$ with the same origin on both sides. Reflection positivity gives nonnegative population diagonal entries for these symmetric insertions. The reflected form has a boundary state induced by the open slab. A small $d_\ell/d$ describes this particular origin-dependent difference in that preparation. The difference generally is not the fine conditional residual $QF$, and the three readings do not bound the operator norm on all new states. [Open-boundary transfer formula, Lüscher and Schaefer, Eq. 2.13](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1105.4749#page=6)

The sampler regenerates the original hot and cold streams and writes the parity-resolved readings at both times. The verifier compares their mean to the archived bare winding reading configuration by configuration. Estimates pool the chain moments first and recompute the ratios with each chain left out. The errors measure variation among four production streams; two shorter pilot streams per size are tabulated separately by the executable. Neither set gives certified joint confidence bounds. The retained winding average alone becomes a worse predictor at finer spacing in these three ensembles. After a physical time $\ell$, its origin-dependent difference has a much smaller central ratio than the full reading at each spacing. A single linear predictor gives an upper bound on the optimal conditional loss at equal time; its observed deterioration supplies no lower bound on that optimal loss. All 26,496 reconstructed full-component readings from the 18 pilot and production chains match the original float32 archive exactly. The two-stream pilots show larger fluctuations in the physical-time ratios.

At each of the eight origins the ordered coarse-link products telescope to the winding holonomy based on that skeleton. G4 also formed those two-link products explicitly on inhomogeneous fields, passed the coarse fields to Z4’s box routine and matched each origin separately against the native fine-lattice reader. All components passed before and after site gauge rotations on sides 6, 8 and 10. B4’s reflected winding–clover analysis uses different observables and the same open-boundary distinction.
