# Winding normalization and a low-spin state lost under blocking

Z4’s new two-site code uses different first operators from the one-site code. The distinction matters for signed matrix elements and absolute spectral weights. Write $p_i=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}U_i$, $\mathbf v_i=\operatorname{Im}U_i$, $s_i=1-p_i^2$, $d_{ij}=\mathbf v_i\cdot\mathbf v_j$, with $U_i$ a spatial winding based at the same vertex. G4’s unsmeared cyclic $E$ differences use $(4p_i^2-1)/3$, and its $T_2$ entries use $p_ip_jd_{ij}$ in the order $(23,31,12)$. [G4’s sampler](../../g4/full-attempt/physical-refinement/matched.rs)

|---|---|---|
| Three-direction one-site | $s_i$ | $T_{2,Z}=T_{2,G}$ |
| Multi-site box | Spatial mean of $p_i^2$ | $T_{2,Z}=-4T_{2,G}$ |

The box tensor is the spatial mean of $(p_ip_j-d_{ij})^2-(p_ip_j+d_{ij})^2$. Thus its first $T_2$ autocovariance is **16 times** G4’s on the same fields, and its signed first component has opposite sign. In the $E$ channel the traceless basis changes sign when $s_i$ is replaced by $p_i^2$. Its covariance still satisfies $C_{E,G}=(32/9)C_{E,Z}$ for the respective component averages. In particular G4’s third cyclic $E$ component is $-4\sqrt2/3$ times Z4’s first orthonormal component at one site and $+4\sqrt2/3$ times it for the box definition on the same holonomies. Normalized first-operator autocorrelation ratios cancel these factors whenever the zero-lag denominator is nonzero. Comparisons of different smearings, boundary preparations or full variational bases need their own matching. ## An exact test of the remaining fine sector

In the electric SU(2) theory, the vacuum is product Haar on links before gauge projection. Take an even fine side $N\ge4$ and block adjacent pairs of straight links on the even sublattice. A winding character depends only on its transverse line and is invariant under changing the base along that line. In each direction there are $N^2$ independent Haar windings, with independent sets of links for different directions. Let $v=(\overline{p_1^2}-\overline{p_2^2})/\sqrt2$, the first normalized box component in Z4’s new code. Since $\mathbb E[p^2]=1/4$ and $\mathbb E[p^4]=1/8$, its variance is $1/(16N^2)$.

Exactly $N^2/4$ lines in each direction have two even transverse coordinates. Their full winding traces can be reconstructed from coarse products. All other lines use independent links outside the retained skeleton, so conditional expectation replaces their squared traces by $1/4$. Conditioning on the gauge-orbit algebra gives the same result because the reconstructed functions are gauge invariant. Consequently $\|Pv\|^2/\|v\|^2=1/4$ and **$\|Qv\|^2/\|v\|^2=3/4$ at every refinement**. Although $v$ uses only the spin-one character plus a constant, three quarters of its normalized equal-time variance stays geometrically fine.

For the heat-electric control with fundamental one-link multiplier $e^{-s/N^2}$, the spin-one multiplier is $e^{-8s/(3N^2)}$. Every winding in $Qv$ has length $N$, so at physical time $N$ steps, $S Qv=e^{-8s/3}Qv$. In this control coarse and fine transfer decouple exactly. It follows that $\|QSQ\|\ge e^{-8s/3}$ even though the local representation cutoff already contains this state. The elementary plaquette gives the still stronger closing-gap counterexample in the earlier work. This calculation pinpoints a surviving low-spin contribution for the observable used in the cross-director comparison. It rules out transporting the entire quadratic-model decay argument using only spin cutoff and geometry. The isotropic vacuum weights and dynamics must enter.
