# An isotropic bound and where the one-link argument breaks

At small bare coupling the unfiltered sum admits an explicit volume-independent bound,
$$
 \chi_{N,\beta}\le \frac{8\beta^2}{1-18\tanh\beta},\qquad
 0<\beta<\operatorname{atanh}(1/18).
$$
This is a strong-coupling control at an actual isotropic Wilson point. The matched weak-coupling trajectory in this program has $\beta>3$. The worst-case single-link contraction required by this proof fails for **every** $\beta\ge1$.

Start on a finite periodic Wilson cylinder. Let $D_{ef}$ be the supremum total variation distance between conditional laws of link $e$ when only exterior link $f$ changes. If $m_{ef}$ plaquettes contain both, the oscillation of the change in log unnormalized density is at most $4\beta m_{ef}$. For two probability laws with a likelihood ratio whose log oscillation is $L$, total variation is at most $\tanh(L/4)$. Indeed for ratio $r\in[a,b]$ of mean one, the chord bound for $(r-1)_+$ gives $(b-1)(1-a)/(b-a)$; maximizing at $ab=1$ with $b/a=e^L$ gives the formula. Thus a symmetric entrywise majorant is $C_{ef}=m_{ef}\tanh\beta$ for $e\ne f$, with zero diagonal. Each link has six incident faces with three other links each, so $\|C\|_2\le18\tanh\beta=\alpha$.

For completeness let $L$ be the number of links, $P=L^{-1}\sum_e P_e$ random-scan heat bath and $v_e(f)$ the oscillation when varying only link $e$. Maximal coupling yields $v(Pf)\le Bv(f)$ entrywise with $B=(1-1/L)I+C/L$. For resampling $e$, variation in another coordinate gives its original oscillation plus $C_{ef}v_e(f)$; resampling the varied coordinate erases its influence. When $\alpha<1$, iteration and telescoping over the links give uniform convergence $P^k f\to\mathbb Ef$ at fixed finite volume. Since each $P_e$ is conditional expectation, $\langle (I-P)f,g\rangle$ is the average expected conditional covariance, bounded in absolute value by $(4L)^{-1}v(f)\cdot v(g)$. Summing with $g=P^k f$ gives $\operatorname{Var} f\le\|v(f)\|_2^2/[4(1-\alpha)]$. For the unnormalized scaled sum on one slice, only $2V$ links have nonzero oscillation, at most $4\beta$ each. Divide by $V$ and take the fixed-spatial-volume vacuum limit. [Föllmer, *A Covariance Estimate for Gibbs Measures*](https://www.math.hu-berlin.de/~foellmer/publications.html)

### A Wilson exterior falsifies the contraction premise at weak coupling

Fix one link and set the connecting orthogonal links of its six staples to identity. Each opposite parallel link belongs to just one of these staples. Choose the target staple to be $+1$ or $-1$ by varying that one opposite link. Assign the other five the fifth roots of unity in a quaternionic circle, whose sum vanishes. This is a legitimate exterior on an even cylinder of periods at least six, with no boundary twist. Its two conditional laws have densities proportional to $\exp(\pm\beta u_0)$. Testing with $u_0\in[-1,1]$ gives total variation at least $R(\beta)=I_2(\beta)/I_1(\beta)$. The mean $R$ is increasing since its derivative is a variance. The positive Bessel recurrence gives $R(1)=1/(4+I_3(1)/I_2(1))>1/(4+1/6)=6/25$. The same construction works for each of the six opposite links, varying the fixed exterior separately for each supremum. Hence the actual row sum of $D$ exceeds $36/25$ at $\beta\ge1$. The exteriors can be approximated on sets of positive measure at each finite coupling by continuity and full support. Improving the elementary upper bound on $D$ cannot rescue this row-sum proof there. This obstruction concerns a supremum over backgrounds. It does not show large variance in the Wilson vacuum. [Bessel recurrence](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.29)

A second construction gives failure even for exteriors admitting a completion with every scaled plaquette cost at most two. [Scaled changes around the flat field](weak-exteriors.md)

### Pressure along the isotropic line

At isotropic spatial couplings the $3\times3$ spatial-source Hessian has diagonal $h$ and off-diagonal $k$ by cubic symmetry. Its scalar and orientation-difference eigenvalues are $s=h+2k$ and $d=h-k$, so $h=(s+2d)/3$. Even exact knowledge of $s$ leaves $d$ open. Varying all six couplings also includes temporal sources. As an algebraic gauge-field control on an even four-torus, choose uniformly one of the six orientations $(a,b)$ and set $U_b(x)=(-1)^{x_a}I$, all other links identity. A Haar gauge rotation may be applied. All selected-orientation faces have half-trace $-1$, the other five orientations $+1$. The total action is constant, while the chosen fixed spatial plane sum has variance per spatial site $5\beta^2 V/9$. This tests a deduction from action fluctuations and symmetry; these six backgrounds do not have Wilson Gibbs weights. The Wilson-specific directional variance still requires control. The [pressure bound averaged over anisotropic couplings](../plaquette-covariance/pressure.md) can miss the prescribed diagonal. Monte Carlo studies of the full specific heat resolve physically interesting coupling dependence but measure a different sum. [Original SU(2) study](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.45.1755)
