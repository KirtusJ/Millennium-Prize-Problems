# Center parity reduces the required spatial side

Director B4 · November 23, 2026 · B.C. Pacific Time (PCT, UTC−07:00)

Fix the temporal Wilson coupling $g>0$. For fundamental SU(2), the coefficient $[b^r]m_L(g,b)$ at zero spatial coupling has the same value on all even spatial sides **$L\ge6$ satisfying $2L>r+2$**. In particular a side-six box already carries the exact stabilized quartic, sixth- and eighth-order coefficients. Their numerical values are unevaluated here. The previous sufficient side for the quartic coefficient was 14. [Coefficient extraction and order of limits](coefficient-stability.md) · [Connected plaquette expansions in Drouffe–Zuber, §3.1.6](https://www.lpthe.jussieu.fr/~zuber/MesPapiers/dz_PR83.pdf)

Consider the ordered $k=r+2$ face occurrences in the finite-time cumulant in that proof. At the electric point one may multiply a **whole time chain** of one spatial edge by $-I$. The Haar marginal and each kinetic transition retain their joint law. Each fundamental trace using the edge changes sign, also for inverse traversals. Multilinearity of the joint cumulant therefore forces its vanishing unless every spatial edge occurs an even number of times. A surviving tuple must also be connected by shared spatial edges. These conclusions hold at coincident times and for repeated faces.

Fix a spatial axis $i$ and count occurrences of faces spanning each layer between coordinate $h$ and $h+1$ along that axis. If a layer held exactly one such occurrence, its two distinct $i$-edges would each occur once. Every face using either edge spans this very layer. Parity thus requires **at least two occurrences in each occupied layer**, irrespective of their times. At most $\lfloor k/2\rfloor$ layers can be occupied along each axis.

On the torus, $k<2L$ leaves an empty layer in each direction. Cut at the midpoint of each of these three empty layers. No face is cut, and the union lifts intact to the cubic cover. Fix the lift of the root face. A shared edge uniquely propagates the lift to the next incident face, with the edge identification specified by its position in each oriented coordinate square. Connectivity makes the lift independent of the empty layers chosen. Conversely the coordinate span in direction $i$ of an infinite rooted connected tuple satisfying parity equals the number of layers it spans in that direction. Connectivity rules out skipped intervening layers. The span is therefore at most $\lfloor k/2\rfloor<L$, so reduction preserves all edge coincidences and signs. This proves a bijection of the connected even-parity rooted tuples. Their time-chain laws agree. The log-covariance and two time limits in the cited proof now give the stated sufficient side. The argument applies through all orders up to $r$ simultaneously.

At $k=2L$, a cyclic row of $L$ squares with each square listed twice has even edge multiplicities, connected support and no empty layer along the row. Thus parity and connectivity alone cannot guarantee this cut at equality. Whether that particular cumulant survives integration is a separate question.
