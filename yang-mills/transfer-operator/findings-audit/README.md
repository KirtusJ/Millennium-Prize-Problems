Director B4 · November 23, 2026 · B.C. Pacific Time (PCT, UTC−07:00)

The fine ensemble contains **834 distinct configurations in 840 frozen rows** after the ten initial exclusions per seed. ## Effect on the estimates

Parentheses give one estimated standard error in the final digits. The historical row reproduces the published estimator. The two corrected rows use all distinct observations in their selection. Their errors use 4,000 circular moving-block bootstrap draws with length 25 inside each seed. The coarse and middle estimates retain their original blocks throughout.

| Fine selection | $Q_0$ | $R(0)$ | $10^3 R(4)$ | $10^3 R(5)$ | $10^3 R(6)$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Historical published | 0.09138(46) | 0.03233(61) | 1.37(50) | 0.09(59) | −0.23(69) |
| All 834 distinct frozen | 0.09139(45) | 0.03235(68) | 1.29(51) | −0.04(63) | −0.35(69) |
| All 840 declared IDs | 0.09142(45) | 0.03248(67) | 1.31(51) | −0.08(64) | −0.44(70) |

All 16 point estimates pass the original one-sided compatibility rule $R\ge-2\,\mathrm{SE}$. Fourteen have $R-2\,\mathrm{SE}>0$. These counts also hold with seed-separated whole blocks of 17, 25 and 50, and with circular blocks of 50. They are pointwise diagnostics. The middle $Q_0$ lies 13.7 estimated errors below the line through the two outer spacings for both corrected all-row samples at block length 25. The corresponding deviations for $R(0)$ are much closer to the two-error cutoff, at +2.22 and +2.13. All three pairwise fine-seed mean comparisons stay within 1.1 estimated errors under both corrected selections.

For the 834 distinct frozen configurations, the fine-to-comparison ratios at box fractions 1/6, 1/4 and 1/3 are 0.579(47), 0.613(101) and 0.353(144). They compare different pairs of lattices. The sides in string-tension units are 2.214, 2.128 and 2.286, so the two quarter-box separations are 0.5320 and 0.5715 in those units. The ratios do not determine a decrease per refinement at one fixed physical separation. The normalized flow time relative to the box also entails different physical flow times. [Fingberg, Heller and Karsch, Table 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-lat/9208012#page=14)
