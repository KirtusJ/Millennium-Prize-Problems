# Verification, numerical controls and scope

The proof uses the original positive Wilson transfer, with one temporal gauge integral per vertex and a fixed ratio of temporal to spatial spacings. Anchored gauge fixing leaves both the chord kinetic covariance and the residual color projection to be dealt with explicitly. Harmonic approximation of isolated nondegenerate wells is classical; Simon's theorem concerns Schrödinger operators. Chatterjee used axial gauge and Gaussian comparison to identify the leading Yang–Mills free energy. The mathematical notes here give a kernel and vacuum-relative error for the compact discrete-time transfer and for the time-separated observables. Periodic spatial boundary conditions have flat holonomies and require a separate analysis. [Lüscher’s transfer](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01614090) · [Simon on nondegenerate wells](https://www.numdam.org/item/AIHPA_1983__38_3_295_0/) · [Chatterjee on the leading free energy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.01222) · [Lüscher’s periodic small-volume problem](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(83)90436-4)

The 22 Lean declarations cover the transverse curl symbol, the tree kinetic matrix identity and the bound obtained by counting chords through each face, the Mehler quadratic relation, color and singlet algebra, the exact one-cube matrix polynomial, the two-sided trace-angle algebra, tested integral domination and finite positive path products. They also check the Wick color factors and the positive-energy Laplace integral of $E^7$. The Gaussian operator limit, compact group representation argument, growing-box estimates, complete thermal traces and positive-time continuum limits are proofs in the mathematical notes. No axiom asserting a continuum theory is added.

The numerical checks have three independent roles.

- Integer incidence matrices on open boxes of one to four cells per side verify $dC=d_1$. Full real-matrix eigenvalues match the separated-mode list at every nonzero level. Omitting the kinetic tree noise fails already on one cube.
- A single original compact SU(2) square can be checked in the character basis with class angle $\alpha$. The half-weight-sandwiched transfer has the same positive spectrum as the electric-square-root representation. Its matrix there is $(I_d(\beta_t)I_e(\beta_t)/I_1(\beta_t)^2)^2 e^{-\beta_s}[I_{|d-e|}(\beta_s)-I_{d+e}(\beta_s)]$. A normalized deficit insertion is obtained by differentiating the magnetic integral. At three anisotropies and four couplings, the low singlet gap and the vacuum-to-first deficit weight approach $4\operatorname{arsinh}(\eta)$ and $6/(1+\eta^2)$ for this single square. The positive discarded kinetic spectrum in the original split-weight representation is bounded by the fourth power of the first omitted Bessel ratio. The floating-point tail estimates appear in the read-back. [Character check](controls.py)
- On one actual three-dimensional SU(2) cube a separate program evaluates the compact bond integral on the Gaussian vacuum trial and six color-singlet quadratic trials. It includes all twelve temporal increments, both endpoint Jacobians, both spatial actions and seven tree-link integrals. Conditional Gaussian sampling supplies the reference bond exactly; pairing positive and negative $g$ cancels odd sampling noise. For two fixed seeds, the sampled normalized matrix discrepancy at $g=0.0225$ is approximately $0.00148$ and $0.00106$. The ratio between the first two discrepancies in one seed was $0.352$, just above a preliminary $0.35$ cutoff; the finest halving gives approximately $0.25$ in both seeds. These are coupled numerical diagnostics of the kernel on trial functions. They have no certification role for the compact eigenvalues. [Compact bond test](cube_control.py)

Direct finite-box frequency sums check the interior curvature kernel at coincidence and at displacements parallel and perpendicular to the measured magnetic component, including the change of sign in the unsquared covariance. A separate SU(2) class-angle integral checks the shrinking center neighborhoods in the thermal trace. At $N=127$ the integrated singlet fraction times $8\sqrt\pi S_N^{3/2}$ is approximately $1.00342$. The formal results and the limiting proofs do not require these numerical approximations.
