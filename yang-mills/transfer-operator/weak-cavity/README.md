# The weak-coupling Wilson transfer in an open spatial box

Director B4 · Yang–Mills existence and mass gap · November 22 research session, continued November 23, 2026 · B.C. Pacific Time (PCT, UTC−07:00)

On an open cube with $N$ cells per side, the **full gauge-invariant Wilson transfer** has a computable weak-coupling spectrum. This includes the original isotropic action. Take a fixed compact connected group with nonabelian simple Lie algebra, a faithful unitary representation, spatial spacing $b$, temporal spacing $a=\eta b$ with fixed $0<\eta<\infty$, and Wilson couplings $\beta_s=\eta/g^2$, $\beta_t=1/(\eta g^2)$. Use the representation's normalized real trace in both Wilson terms. Let $M=N+1$. At fixed $N$, as $g\downarrow0$, the six lowest excited levels have a common limiting energy above the vacuum:
$$
 a(E_1-E_0),\ldots,a(E_6-E_0)\ \longrightarrow 4\operatorname{arsinh}\!\left(\eta\sqrt2\sin\frac{\pi}{2M}\right).
$$
A single transverse quantum transforms in the adjoint and is absent from the physical space. The first six states are two-quantum color singlets. Three are detected by ordinary elementary plaquette actions. Three more are detected by comparing the two orientations of a loop traversing two perpendicular faces. There is a joint estimate on the original compact transfer, including its vacuum normalization. For the first gap it suffices that $gN^{19}\to0$. One explicit trajectory is $g=N^{-100}$ and $b=N^{-1/2}$. The physical box grows, both lattice spacings vanish, and the true first gap is asymptotic to $2\sqrt2\,\pi/\sqrt N$. Keeping $Mb=R$ fixed instead gives the finite cavity gap $2\sqrt2\,\pi/R$. [Volume and time estimates](uniform.md)

The gapless signal also occurs in the interior. On the growing-box trajectory a centered spatial plaquette with the normalization $2/(g^2 b^4)$ applied to its half-trace deficit for SU(2), or normalized-real-trace deficit for general $G$, has positive-time autocorrelation
$$
                       C(t)=\frac{2\dim G}{\pi^4 t^8},\qquad t>0.
$$
Its spectral measure has density $2\dim G\,E^7/(7!\pi^4)$ down to zero. Choosing $g=e^{-N}$ on the same boxes gives every fixed time-separated moment of this observable as a Wick square of free magnetic curvature. The three-point function is nonzero in that Gaussian theory. [Local spectral measure and higher moments](bulk.md)

These results identify the free limit of a family with a prescribed rapidly falling bare coupling. The physical scale generated by interacting four-dimensional Yang–Mills is undetermined on the intended massive trajectory. The Clay existence and mass-gap claim remains open. With periodic Euclidean time the full compact physical heat trace also converges on these trajectories. Its growing-box pressure is $D\pi^2/(45T^4)$ at inverse temperature $T$. Projection onto the global color singlet costs a factor of order $R^{-3D/2}$ relative to leaving the root color unprojected on a box of physical side $R$. The coefficient is explicit.
