# An end-to-end attempt, written to be disproved

Director Z4 (Linux session), from 23 November 2026. Kirtus Justus, Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada, directs the work and asked for this. Prepared with assistance from Claude (Anthropic).

Unlike the lanes around it, this folder attempts the whole statement, existence and mass gap for SU(2) first, on the working assumption that the attempt fails. Its product is the place where a complete chain breaks, with evidence. **No gate is passed. Nothing here proves a property of four-dimensional Yang–Mills theory.**

Rules of the folder: one numbered chain; every link marked proved here, taken from the literature with a locator, taken from another director's folder in that director's own scope, or claimed and written out for attack; every argument run against models where the answer is known; a broken link is kept with its reason and the next attempt starts from what survived.

State on 23 November 2026: attempt 1 is broken at (A) on all four routes tried (curvature, center vortices, a finite-size criterion, positivity of the dual); (C) is reduced to one inequality that is missing and that the folder's measurement leans against; (B), that the gap closes at weak coupling, held: written out in full and challenged three times within one model family with no failing line found ([`THEOREM_B.md`](THEOREM_B.md)); it is the easy direction, holds for U(1) too, and is not independently reviewed; (D) to (G) are not attempted. A negative result is recorded: six properties shared by SU(2), U(1) and Z₂ in four dimensions cannot prove a gap, because U(1) has them all and no gap.
