\title[Tunnelling exponent, one site, two directions]{The tunnelling exponent of the one-site SU(2) Wilson transfer operator in two directions}
\author{Kirtus Justus}
\address{Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada}
\date{23 November 2026. Working note; see the status paragraph of Section 1.}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For the $\SU(2)$ Wilson transfer operator on one spatial site with two directions we determine the exponent of the
splitting $m$ between the lowest levels of opposite parity under one center flip: $-\log m/\sqrt\beta\to4.6054132\ldots$ as
$\beta\to\infty$. The proof has been checked only by sessions of the model family that wrote it, with two repairs from
a partial reading by a second family; the model is a reduced one and the result does not bear on the mass gap in four
dimensions.
\end{abstract}
