\title[Tunnelling exponent, one site, two directions]{The tunnelling exponent of the one-site SU(2) Wilson transfer operator in two directions}
\author{Kirtus Justus}
\address{Theoretical Physicist, Port Coquitlam, British Columbia, Canada}
\date{23 November 2026. Working note; see the status paragraph of Section 1.}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For the $\SU(2)$ Wilson transfer operator on one spatial site with two directions, let $m$ be the splitting
between the lowest levels of opposite parity under one center flip. We give a proposed proof that
$-\log m/\sqrt\beta\to\int_0^\pi\sqrt{4\operatorname{arsinh}(\sin a)}\,da=4.6054132\ldots$ as $\beta\to\infty$. Six gaps
found when the argument was first written out have received written repairs; the whole has been checked only
by sessions of the model family that wrote it, with two further repairs from a partial reading by a second
family. The model is a reduced one and the result does not bear on the mass gap in four dimensions.
\end{abstract}
