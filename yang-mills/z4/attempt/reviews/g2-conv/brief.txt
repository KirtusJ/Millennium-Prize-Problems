The model (two spatial directions; one spatial site; infinite time). Configuration (U_1, U_2) in SU(2)^2, Haar measure. Transfer operator T_beta = e^{-V/2} K e^{-V/2} on L^2 of functions invariant under simultaneous conjugation, with V = beta (1 - (1/2) Re Tr(U_1 U_2 U_1^{-1} U_2^{-1})) = 2 beta sin^2 a_1 sin^2 a_2 sin^2 gamma (U_i = exp(i a_i n_i . sigma), gamma the angle between n_1 and n_2) and K diagonal in the Peter-Weyl basis with eigenvalue per link e^{-beta} (2/beta) I_{2j+1}(beta) on spin j (so K ~ exp((1/(2 beta)) Laplacian) at low spin). Center flips U_i -> -U_i commute with T.
