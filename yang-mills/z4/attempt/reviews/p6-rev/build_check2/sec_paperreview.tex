Two reviews of the assembled first version were made, both by sessions of the same model family as the writers; neither is independent review.

\emph{Referee report.} The recommendation was major revision. On the referee's line-by-line reading the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} stood, with seven numerical predicates of the referee's own programs met and none failed (the constants $D^*$ and $c_H$, $G(0)$, $G(1)$ and the asymptotics of $G$, the Dirichlet covariances, the cube word, a two-dimensional analogue of the chessboard estimate, and the negative amplitude of Observation~\ref{neg:obs-dual}). The referee found nine defects in the surrounding text: contradictory claims about $\U(1)$, a claimed review that did not exist, a gap between the stated and the proved form of H0, a statement of Section~\ref{sec:CE} that contradicted Section~\ref{sec:proof}, a clash of hypothesis names with a false ``standing assumption'', overstated evidence in the curvature route, the wrong pair of box sizes in the vortex route, an abstract family that was not given, and an abstract and title that hid the main structure result. It required fifteen changes.

\emph{Fidelity review.} A line-by-line comparison of $27$ statements with the working sources found $9$ faithful, $7$ overstated, $2$ wrong, $5$ omitted, $3$ inconsistent and $1$ not checkable against the sources; its own program predicates were $13$ met, $1$ failed (a defect of the paper: the claimed review) and $17$ measured only.

The present text is the revision made in response to both reviews. It has not been re-reviewed.
