## Inflation found

I read READMEs + execution/“open” lines, not every statement file in full, so this list is partial.
- `blocked-time/README.md` headline "volume-independent physical gap for fine Wilson time steps" is true only for β_s=aλ→0 (spatial coupling vanishing with a), z≤1/384, fixed spatial spacing; its own `local-limit.md` states an obstruction under spatial refinement. Title reads stronger than the scope of the body.
- `weak-cavity/README.md` "closing physical gap" and "full spectrum" are about a free Gaussian limit at g→0; not an interacting mass gap (README says so at the end; opening paragraph does not).
- `hamiltonian-time/README.md` table: the explicit 1/384 is for an observable bound; the gap itself rests on an external existence threshold (stated in the table).
- No README claim was found that contradicts its statement file; I did not exhaustively compare.
