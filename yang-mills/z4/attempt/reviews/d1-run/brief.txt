Context (read A1_LEDGER.md and A2_LEDGER.md first). An attempt at the Yang-Mills mass gap has failed at its principal hypothesis on every route, each time at "asymptotic freedom used non-perturbatively". The ledgers record that the only PROVED properties separating SU(2) from U(1) are local (perfectness, positive Ricci curvature, signed character amplitudes) and that everything proved at weak coupling so far (THEOREM_B.md) holds for U(1) as well, because the group enters only through error terms of order beta^{-1/2}. The director wants the NEXT order: a statement, proved, in which the non-abelian group enters with a definite SIGN at weak coupling, and U(1) does not. The obvious candidate is the first coefficient of the running of a finite-volume coupling. It will be perturbative in beta at fixed box size (the limit beta -> infinity is taken first), so it cannot bear on the gap; the director knows that. The aim is to turn "the coupling runs, in perturbation theory" into "Theorem: ..., with this exact order of limits", and to see exactly what is and is not proved.
