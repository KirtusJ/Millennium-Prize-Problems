sec_proof_SU2.tex in this directory is the full proof (LaTeX, a section of a paper; preamble.tex beside it) of this theorem for the gauge group SU(2): for Wilson lattice gauge theory in four dimensions, along infinite-volume limits of periodic states on boxes of side 2^k, the normalized correlation of the rescaled plaquette energy O_p = beta(1 - cos alpha_p) at time separation n is, in the weak-coupling limit, at least (G(n)/G(0))^2 with G the free lattice Maxwell field-strength correlation; hence the gap of the transfer matrix in lattice units tends to zero or is zero. The proof has been challenged three times and read by a referee; treat it as correct unless you find otherwise (and if you do, say so first).

The hypothesis to remove: the gauge group. The official Yang-Mills problem asks for every compact simple gauge group; the paper's authors say the proof uses SU(2) only through explicit constants and error terms of order beta^{-1/2}, and that they "expect" it to extend. Make that a theorem or find where it fails.
