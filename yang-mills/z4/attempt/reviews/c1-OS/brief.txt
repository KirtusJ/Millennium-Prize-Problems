Context (read A1_LEDGER.md first, then A1_REDUCTION.md, THEOREM_B.md, GROUNDING.md). An end-to-end attempt at the Yang-Mills existence and mass gap problem, written to be disproved, has so far a reduction for ONE two-point function: if (A) the infinite-volume lattice theory has a gap at every coupling, (B) the gap closes at weak coupling [now proved as Theorem B' and, by other directors, with a rate], and (C) one local observable keeps bounded energy in units of the gap after one correlation length, then that observable's two-point function has a nonzero gapped scaling limit. The official problem asks for far more: a quantum field theory satisfying the Osterwalder-Schrader or Wightman axioms, with local fields, a unique vacuum, a mass gap, and short-distance agreement with asymptotic freedom. The director now wants ATTEMPT 2: the WHOLE chain written as one conditional theorem, so that the complete list of unproved hypotheses is on the table and each can be tested or attacked separately. (A) is to be taken as a hypothesis here: four routes to it have failed and it is the heart of the problem; do not try to prove it.

Your task. State and prove, as far as it can be proved, a theorem of this form:

  THEOREM S. Let the Wilson SU(2) lattice theories in four dimensions satisfy hypotheses H1, ..., Hk (to be found by you; each a precise statement about the LATTICE theories, uniform along beta -> infinity). Then, with the lattice spacing a(beta) := m(beta) (the lattice gap), there is a sequence beta_j -> infinity along which the rescaled, normalized correlation functions of a specified family of gauge-invariant fields converge to Schwinger functions satisfying the Osterwalder-Schrader axioms (name the version: OS 1973 with the 1975 correction, i.e. E0' linear growth; or Glimm-Jaffe's formulation), so that by the reconstruction theorem there is a Wightman theory with a unique vacuum and a mass gap >= 1 in these units.
