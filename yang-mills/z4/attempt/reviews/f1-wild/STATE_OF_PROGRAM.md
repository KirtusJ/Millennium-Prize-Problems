# What the program's directors report as of 23 November 2026, 16:00 UTC (collected by Director Z4 from the coordination record; statements are theirs, unread by Z4 unless said)

Setting: SU(2) Wilson lattice gauge theory in four dimensions, coupling beta, infinite-volume limits of even periodic boxes at fixed beta ("periodic-limit states"); gap m(beta) of the transfer matrix in lattice units.
- Upper bounds on the gap: m <= C beta^{-1/8} (one group), improved to C beta^{-1/7} (another), for SU(2); C_{G,rho} beta^{-1/8} for every compact Lie group of positive dimension (awaiting review). For each fixed M, at least M linearly independent gauge-invariant vectors with energies in a band of order beta^{-1/8}.
- Local structure: the connected covariance of the single-plaquette energy Y = beta(1 - (1/2)Tr U_p) equals (3/2) G(n,z)^2 up to C beta^{-1/8}, uniformly in the time lag, for spatial offsets up to beta^{1/4} (G the free lattice Maxwell field-strength correlation, ~ 1/(pi^2 n^4)). Informative for separations up to about beta^{1/64}. Block observables: convergence to free block readings for block radius R << beta^{1/8}, with a Gamma-law for the tilted energy in units of the lag; momentum-tail bounds.
- Large-field control: P(O_p > t) <= e^{60.2 - t/8} for one plaquette; for any finite set A of unit cubes, P(every cube of A contains a plaquette with Y > t) <= min(1, e^{60.2 - t/8})^{|A|}, uniformly in beta >= 1 (so clusters of high-action cubes are exponentially rare in their size once t > 481). Exponential moments E exp(3 O_p/10) <= 6^{9/2}. "This controls high-action clusters; mixing across low-action regions and a positive physical gap remain open."
- Conditional structure: in a box of side L with the actual Wilson shell, the mean conditional total variation between the interior link law and a Gaussian centred at the minimizer for that shell is at most C (L^10/sqrt(beta))^{1/2}: convergence for L = o(beta^{1/20}). The far exterior decouples from a fixed central block (total-variation approach to a product law as coupling and enclosing radius diverge), with sharp order R^{-4} for the limiting coefficient.
- Transfer matrix: positive semidefinite on the site-reflection space for Wilson periodic-limit states (bond-plane argument).
- Strong coupling and fixed mesh: gaps uniform in volume at small beta and for anisotropic lattices with small temporal coupling (z <= 1/384) at fixed spatial mesh; all proportional to 1/(spacing) in physical units.
- Fixed periodic box, weak coupling: partition-function asymptotics with torons (inverse-log corrections); a perturbative coefficient -71 kappa/840 in an anisotropic limit that "supplies no uniform remainder reaching the isotropic line".
- One director has begun a second end-to-end attempt: fix the physical unit independently of the gap and try to propagate a contraction of gauge-invariant observables across a slab of fixed physical thickness.
- Negative results: reflection positivity fails for observables smoothed by a four-dimensional gradient flow; (C+) does not imply the regularity hypothesis H3(b) for a specified field; several abstract counterexamples showing that finitely many correlation readings cannot certify a gap.
- Nothing anywhere bounds the gap from below at weak coupling.
