## Defect

**1. Proposition (B), Estimate (B), line 788: DEFECT.**
- **Line:** line 788 says "every real eigenfunction $u$ of $T$ with eigenvalue $\mu>0$".
- **Reason:** the proof needs $\mu\ge e^{-\eta/4}$, not just $\mu>0$.
  - Line 807 uses it in the step "on $I^c$, $\mu-e^q\ge e^{-\eta/4}-e^{-\eta/2}>0$", and again on $I$ in $e^q-\mu\le e^{\eta/2}-e^{-\eta/4}$.
  - If $\mu$ is small, $\mu-e^q$ is negative on $I^c$. The inequality $\int_{I^c}(\mu-e^q)h^2\le\int_I(e^q-\mu)h^2$ then gives no bound on $\int_{I^c}h^2$.
  - The hypothesis only appears afterwards, in the "In particular" sentence (lines 793–794).
- **Severity:** the statement is false as written, but the proof is repaired by changing the hypothesis to "$\mu\ge e^{-\eta/4}$". The later uses are fine, because Theorem `U-main` (line 853) supplies $\lambda,\lambda_{eo}\ge e^{-\eta/4}$ from Lemma `L-L4`. That lemma is in the second half, which I did not check.
