## Defects (most serious first)

1. **DEFECT, lines 504–509, Theorem U-F-master, interior region.** The split at `|W'|² ≤ 2τ` is wrong.
   - On `{|W'|² > 2τ}` with `|w/A| ≤ √τ`, the triangle inequality only gives `|W'−w/A| > (√2−1)√τ ≈ 0.414√τ`.
   - Line 507 claims `> √τ`. That is false, so the tail probability `e^{−βAτ/2}` in the third term of `M_in` is unjustified.
   - The correct tail is `e^{−βA(√2−1)²τ/2}`.
   - Lines 504–505 already use `(1−τ)(1−4τ)`, which needs `r' ≤ 4τ`. The consistent fix is to split at `|W'|² ≤ 4τ`. Then `|W'| > 2√τ` gives `|W'−w/A| > √τ`.
   - This propagates to Corollary U-F-cor(b), Lemma F, Proposition B and Theorem U-main, but it is repairable and doesn't change the limit.

2. **DEFECT, lines 101 and 564–566, numeric claim.** The lower end "7.3" (or "7.33") of the range `100 ≤ β ≤ 4096` is wrong.
   - I evaluated the master-bound deficit exactly as defined, with `τ=δ=β^{-1/3}` and the worst `s` on `[0.1,1]`. It runs from 8.83 at β=100 to 14.23 at β=4096. The minimum over the range is at β=100.
   - The upper end 14.23 is correct.
   - The "corrected by a referee recomputation" note replaced the working file's 8.6 with 7.33, and the true value is 8.83.
   - This is cosmetic. No proof uses it.
