**One defect in Sections 5–8.** Line numbers below are from `tun_lower.tex`.

**DEFECT 1: the strip a₁ ∈ [a₋/2, a₋) is in Ω but is never checked.**
- Line 315 defines Ω with a₁ ∈ [a₋/2, π−a₋/2]. The tube 𝒯 (lines 360–364) has η′ = a₋, so it starts at a₁ = a₋.
- Line 364 claims 𝒯 ⊇ Ω∩{a₁ ≤ π/2}. That is false on the strip. It is also the point where Ω first goes wrong, since the claim on line 805 builds on it.
- Line 805 (Prop. L-sub) then says "x ∈ 𝒯, so by Lemma S4". S4 and (E1)–(E3) are only proved for a₁ ≥ a₋. S4 needs χ = 1, i.e. Ψ′²/2 = ε+η (line 709). On the strip χ < 1.
- Lines 786–787 (Lemma L-norm) claim 1_Ω = 1_Ω P with P = 1{a₁ ∈ [a₋, π−a₋]}. That is false on the same strip.
- Lines 342–343 (ansatz-props (iv)) claim a₁ and π−a₁ lie in [a₋, π−a₋] on Ω. That is false on the same strip.
- So hypotheses (i) and (ii) of Lemma L-C are unproved there, and Prop. L-sub, Lemma L-balls (II) and Theorem L-main inherit the gap.
- The note's own text points the other way. Line 924 already uses R_well∩{a₁ < a₋}, and lines 623–631 say the comparison needs a₁ ≥ a₋.
- The likely fix is to define Ω with a₁ ∈ [a₋, π−a₋] and R_well with a₁ < a₋ or a₁ > π−a₋. Then H4cor (line 924) already covers R_well. I did not re-check every downstream use after such a change.
