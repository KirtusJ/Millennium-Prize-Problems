## Defects

1. **Lines 101 and 564–565: the lower end of the deficit range is wrong.**
   - The note says that for 100 ≤ β ≤ 4096 the deficit is "7.3 to 14.2 nats" (line 101) and "7.33 to 14.23" (line 564–565).
   - I evaluated the Theorem~F-master formula with τ=δ=β^{-1/3}, taking the maximum over s∈[0.1,1] on a 101-point grid, and scanned β from 100 to 4096.
   - The upper end reproduces: 14.2298 at β=4096, s=1.
   - The lower end does not. The minimum over the range is 8.83, at β=100, s=1, and the deficit only rises with β. 7.33 never occurs.
   - Remark 566 says the working file had "8.6 to 14" and that a referee "corrected" it. The correction moved the figure the wrong way. 8.83 matches the original 8.6 far better than 7.33 does.
   - This is a numerical remark only. No proof uses it.

2. **Line 277, nit: the symbol K is redefined.**
   - Line 22 sets K=K₁K₂ on L²(X). Line 277 says "where K is convolution by k" on L²(SU(2)), and Lemma R(4), Lemma F and the master bound then use that K.
   - The argument stays correct, but the same symbol means two different operators. The one-link operator needs its own name.
