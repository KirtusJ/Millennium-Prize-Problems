## Defects

1. **Line 947 (the choice M² := M_A²(η) + log β) is inconsistent with the rest of the note and breaks the tail estimate.** DEFECT.
   - Lines 962–972 say every threshold and constant is a function of η alone and that M is "now fixed". Line 963 lists only β^{-1/8} and L as β-dependent parameters. Line 581 says the tail error is small "with M fixed". Line 958 says any fixed C would do.
   - Lemma L-tails(b) needs the prefactor e^{(h-2)|b|²+e(S)−Ψ'²/2}·Q to go to 0. On 𝒯, |b|² can be as large as S·M²/√(1+S²), and h−2 = √(1+S²)/S − 1 is always positive. So the prefactor is about β^{1−S/√(1+S²)}, which is roughly β^{0.6} at S ≈ sinh 0.4.
   - Q ≲ e^{-2L²/π²} = β^{-2C/π²}, which is β^{-0.2} for C = 1 (line 958).
   - The product is therefore large, not O(β^{-c}) as lines 612 and 580 claim. That breaks Δ₃ in Proposition L-E13, and so Lemma S4 and the proof of Proposition L-sub.
   - The fix is to take M = M_A(η) fixed, or to choose C large enough in terms of M. Both choices need the rest of the note restated.

2. **Line 769 (the side remark that the old c₁ condition implies (M_A)) has an inequality chain that fails.** DEFECT, minor, and not used by the main proof.
   - The chain "2.65 + 3.61/η < 4.53/η" holds only for η < 0.347. At η = 0.4 it reads 11.675 < 11.325, which is false.
   - Going from 4.53/η to 4.656/sinh η needs sinh η ≤ 1.0278η, not the 1.03η the line cites.
   - The implication itself is probably true, since line 770 reports a numeric check. The derivation as written is not valid.
   - This matters slightly because line 947 relies on the implication ("the earlier choice … implies it").
