   - That holds only for a₁ ≤ π/2. The valley balls have a₁(z) anywhere in [0,π], and for a₁ > π/2 monotonicity gives Ψ(a₁) ≥ Ψ(π/2).
   - The bound g(z) ≥ (μ/4)e^{-1.5ρ_B²}e^{-√βΨ(π/2)} is still true. The fix is to use φ ≥ φ_R for a₁ > π/2, which gives exponent Ψ(π−a₁) ≤ Ψ(π/2), or to cite the tent form of Lemma `tu:L-ansatz-props`(iv).
   - Every ingredient for that fix is already on the page, and Theorem `tu:L-main` is unaffected.
2. **DEFECT (minor), lines 73–74 (Lemma `tu:L-C`).**
   - "If g and φ are continuous, φ ≤ g everywhere" needs every nonempty open set to have positive measure.
   - The lemma is stated for a general σ-finite (X,dx) and does not assume that. Haar measure on SU(2)² has this property, so the application is fine, but the hypothesis is missing from the statement.
