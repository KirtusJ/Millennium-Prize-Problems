YOUR CLAIM: (A) of A1_REDUCTION.md for SU(2), d = 4, infinite volume: the Wilson lattice theory has a positive gap m(beta) > 0 at EVERY beta (equivalently exponential decay of connected correlations of gauge-invariant local observables, with a rate uniform in the volume).
YOUR MECHANISM: positive curvature. The known fact (Shen-Zhu-Zhu; Bakry-Emery): on SU(N)^E the Ricci curvature N/2 of each factor dominates the Hessian of the Wilson action when |beta| < 1/(16(d-1)), giving log-Sobolev and Poincare inequalities uniform in the volume and, from them, exponential decay. Your task is to push this to all beta. Routes you may combine, and you must choose and commit:
  (a) Multiscale: integrate out fluctuations at fixed block variables; show the conditional (fluctuation) measures satisfy a uniform Poincare inequality and the marginal of the block variables is again a lattice gauge measure with a smaller effective beta; iterate until the Bakry-Emery regime; combine with a two-scale criterion (note fact 8: the Grunewald-Otto-Villani-Westdickenberg criterion is proved on linear spaces, so you need a version on SU(2)^E: state it and prove or CLAIM it).
  (b) Averaged curvature: the Hessian of the action is large and negative only on configurations of exponentially small probability (plaquettes far from the identity); replace the pointwise Bakry-Emery condition by an integrated one (for instance a Lyapunov-function or Holley-Stroock-type perturbation argument, or a Poincare inequality via a weighted Bochner formula). State the integrated criterion you use as a theorem with proof or citation.
  (c) Hamiltonian form: the Kogut-Susskind Hamiltonian on L^2(SU(2)^links) restricted to gauge-invariant functions; ground state exp(-Phi); gap >= (g^2/2a) inf (Ric + 2 Hess Phi) by Bakry-Emery; Singer's positive curvature of the orbit space. Then you need a lower bound on Hess Phi.
 Also required: how exponential decay with a rate, or a gap of the transfer matrix, follows from the functional inequality you obtain (the rate in lattice units; LIT_RIGOROUS R5(a) says how Shen-Zhu-Zhu do it).
 For the TEST (iii): a natural one is the exact spectral gap or Poincare constant of a very small system (one plaquette, or a 2x2 periodic two-dimensional lattice, or a single link in a fixed environment) as a function of beta, against what your first CLAIMED step predicts; another is whether Ric + Hess can be negative on a set of non-negligible measure at large beta.
