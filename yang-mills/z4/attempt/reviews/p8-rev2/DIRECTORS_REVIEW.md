## Corrections required
- C1 (groups A, B, C; comments 7105447358, 7105348719, 7105100783). The forest gauge recursion is printed with two group factors reversed. "With the paper's gauge convention the forest recursion is g_y = g_x U_xy." - C2 (group A, comments 7105447358, 7104999220). "The paper's site-reflection space contains an explicit nonzero open-link vector of squared norm 1/4 killed by one time step. Averaging boundary gauge transformations gives an orthogonal projection P with PT = T = TP. The transfer norm on the vacuum complement survives this physical projection." Consequence: T is not injective on the site-reflection Hilbert space, so writing T = exp(-H) there is wrong; the gap defined as -log of the norm of T on the orthocomplement of the vacuum is unaffected. Required: in Section 3 and Section 4, either pass to the physical (gauge-averaged) space, or use the even-lag formulation; do not write T = e^{-H} on the site-reflection space.
- C3 (group C, comment 7105014262, an audit of commit c705f26). "R''s five numbered conclusions hold under the stated hypotheses. Three changes affect how the paper applies them and formulates Theorem S."
   1. "Correct the two sentences after R'. With transfer eigenvalues 1, e^{-m}, e^{-2m} and a unit observable vector entirely in the last mode, (A)-(C) all hold as m -> 0. Yet C(xi) = e^{-2} at every coupling and the vector misses the gap eigenspace entirely." (So: remove the claims that the normalization C(xi) tends to zero and that the chosen observable overlaps the lowest mode.) "For a fixed continuous local gauge-invariant Wilson observable, the paper's plaquette tail does separately imply vanishing variance by flatness on a fixed contractible box."
   2. "Use even lattice separations on the full site-reflection space. ... I proved R''s positive-time subsequential conclusion using only two even clock measurements, a nonzero denominator and the ratio bound. The normalized odd lags have the same limit at every t > c exactly when the fraction of negative spectral weight tilted at the even clock tends to zero."
   3. "Specify the support metric in S. With the usual nearest-neighbour graph distance, the radius-one ball is a nine-vertex tree. Every gauge-invariant observable on its eight edges is constant, making H3(a) impossible. Specifying maximum-coordinate distance admits the 24 plaquettes and preserves Step 6's one-slice buffer."
