# T15 report: is closure(<W4, R_1rad>) = O(4)?

**Verdict: G = O(4): YES.** Evidence: the powers of R are dense in the SO(2) of the (1,2)-plane. The coordinate-plane SO(2)'s generate SO(4), and W4 contains a reflection (det -1). The 5 Haar samples were approximated to Frobenius error 6e-5 to 2.5e-4.

## Proof sketch
1. theta/pi is irrational, so by Kronecker {n*theta mod 2pi} is dense, and the closure of <R> is SO(2)_{12}.
2. Conjugating by a permutation matrix P in W4 gives P SO(2)_{12} P^T = SO(2)_{ij} for every coordinate plane. G contains all six.
3. G is closed, so it is a Lie subgroup. Its Lie algebra contains E_ij - E_ji for all i<j. These six matrices are linearly independent (rank 6, by the grandchild), and dim so(4) = 6 (control: 6). So Lie(G) = so(4).
4. SO(4) is connected, so it is generated by a neighbourhood of the identity. Hence SO(4) is contained in G.
5. W4 contains diag(-1,1,1,1) with det -1, so G meets the other component of O(4) and G = O(4).
