(Assume (A) if you need it, and say so; (B) is also meaningful as "for every eps > 0 there is beta_0 such that for beta > beta_0 the transfer matrix has spectrum in (0, eps) on the orthocomplement of the vacuum, or no gap at all".)
SUGGESTED MECHANISM (yours to change, but commit): an upper bound on the gap from slow decay at fixed distance. For a unit vector psi orthogonal to the vacuum, <psi, T^n psi> <= exp(-m n). So if for some family of slice observables the normalized connected correlation at time separation n satisfies liminf_{beta -> infinity} C_beta(n)/C_beta(0) >= c(n) with -log c(n)/n -> 0 as n -> infinity, then limsup m(beta) = 0. The lower bound on correlations at fixed distance as beta -> infinity is a weak-coupling limit at fixed lattice distance: the claim is that the infinite-volume expectation of fixed local observables, suitably rescaled by powers of beta, converges to that of the free (Gaussian) lattice gauge field with three colours, whose plaquette-plaquette correlation decays like a power of n. INDEX_EXTRACT lists what the other directors have of this kind (list (b): limits toward a free theory, mostly at fixed box or along joint limits; Z5 volume-uniformity; NEG 8). The difficulty is uniformity in the volume: the fixed-box limit is elementary (Laplace method), the infinite-volume one is not.
