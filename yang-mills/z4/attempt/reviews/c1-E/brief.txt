The sharpest known warning is the four-dimensional phi^4 / Ising model: its scaling limit is proved to be Gaussian (Aizenman and Duminil-Copin, Annals of Math. 2021; earlier Aizenman 1981 and Frohlich 1982 in d > 4). The three-dimensional compact U(1) theory is a second warning: Gopfert and Mack prove a gap at all couplings and that the continuum limit at fixed Debye mass is a FREE field.
