## T1 Laplacian normalisation
SU(2) = {a0 + i a.sigma, |a|=1} = unit S^3 in R^4 (quaternions). Left-invariant field Y_j generated by
i*sigma_j: (Y_j)_U = U (i sigma_j); i sigma_j is a unit quaternion and left multiplication by unit U
is an isometry of R^4, so |Y_j| = 1 and Y_j are orthonormal (also mutually orthogonal: i sigma_j
are orthogonal imaginary quaternions). Hence round unit Laplacian = sum_j Y_j^2 (left-invariant
frame, Killing-compatible, divergence-free). Check: spectrum of -Lap on S^3 is l(l+2) = 4 j(j+1).
Convention A: X_j = Y_j/2 => sum_j X_j^2 = Lap/4, -kA sum X^2 = (kA/4)(-Lap).
Match to (1/(2kB))(-Lap): kA/4 = 1/(2kB)  =>  kA = 2/kB.
g = lam/kA = lam kB/2 = z/2.
