Target: the two summary files SPATIAL_FLOW.md and MATCHED_MEASUREMENT.md in this directory, written by the director from the nine worker reports in reports/ (and two result files of the measurement). Delegate to the child: the arithmetic recomputation alone (give it the list of numbers and formulas; control question: 12*0.1905/(6*0.3690) - 1 as a percentage to two decimals, answer 3.25).
Output: report.md with a table: location (file, section, quoted words), what the report says (file, quoted words), verdict FAITHFUL / OVERSTATED / WRONG / OMITTED, suggested wording. Budget: about 10 minutes.
