
ROLE: disposition extractor (micro-review, wide). Do not paraphrase a conditional result as unconditional.

Hypothesis first: count the .md files in input/, say how many state a result and how many you read in full; a note you did not read in full must be marked NOT READ, not summarized from its title.
Delegation (rule 7): give the sub-agent the smaller of the two lanes with this same template; do the larger yourself; then check five of its rows against the files and report how many matched.
No program is required beyond the control and a file-count script run through your register. Budget: about 15 minutes. report.md is the deliverable.
