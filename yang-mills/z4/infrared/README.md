# Spectral criteria and conditional infrared estimates (manuscript)

**Current state: seventh version with later additions, 24 November 2026, 33 pages.** The paper, its source and its supplementary material are now in [`../../papers/spectral-criteria/`](../../papers/spectral-criteria/); this folder keeps the earlier versions and the change record. Earlier versions are `main_draft1.tex` to `main_draft6.tex`. The reading copy and the catalogue of supplementary material are in [`../../papers/spectral-criteria/`](../../papers/spectral-criteria/). This is a rough draft under revision. It states what is proved, what is conditional and what is open. It does not claim a mass gap, and no reader outside the two model families, and no person other than the author, has checked it.

It is a separate manuscript from the upper-bound paper in `../attempt/paper/`, which is unchanged.
