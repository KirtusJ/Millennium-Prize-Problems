- **Remark 5.8, where the infrared problem sits (24 November, later; 37 pages).** `d ≤ b* ≤ (1 + 2/(mγ))d + κ` for the true deficit `b*` of the coarse part. - **Proposition 5.9, a one-step criterion (24 November, later; 38 pages).** For `H ≥ 0`, a projection `P` and `t > 0`: if `‖(I − P)f‖² ≤ κ⟨f, Hf⟩` and `‖P e^{−tH} P‖ ≤ 1 − b` then `1 − ‖e^{−tH}‖ ≥ b/(1 + κ/t)`; conversely a gap `Δ` gives both with `κ = 1/Δ`. So a uniform gap in a box is equivalent to two regulator-uniform statements for coarse variables of one's choice, with no second lattice, no matched coupling and no budget; the second follows from a minorisation of the two-time law of finitely many coarse variables. Proof: Theorem 1.2 with `B = 0` and many small steps. Tested on 20,000 random instances and 2,000 chains (`supplement/S13-weight-step/onestepcrit.py`, three predicates met).
