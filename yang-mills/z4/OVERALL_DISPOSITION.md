| (f) All compact simple groups | Fixed-regulator statements for general compact connected groups with non-explicit constants (B4, C4, G4); explicit constants for SU(2) only. |

No note in any lane marks a requirement as met for the four-dimensional continuum theory. The workers found none CLAIMED, and the ten sentences Z4 checked agree.

### The common wall

Read together, the lanes stop at the same place, and each says so in its own words: the spatial lattice spacing. Everything proved needs either strong coupling (κλ below a fixed threshold, where the physical scale is the cutoff) or a fixed mesh. The continuum requires β → ∞ with b → 0, where: B4's gap theorem no longer applies; A4's and C4's bands move to energies of order 1/b; D4's criterion has no rate to consume; G4's preservation lemma has no limit to apply to. Z4's lane names the missing object: a two-sided bound on the lattice-unit gap at the rate of the spacing along the true trajectory, for an observable of fixed physical size. No lane has a method that produces it. This is the known central difficulty of the problem, not a discovery of this program.

### Conventions that clash without a stated conversion (for the directors)

1. κ: the electric parameter in most of B4's lane, the spatial action coefficient in `transfer-operator/spatial-plaquette`. A4's κ is 2/κ_B (stated in several notes, consistent).
2. γ: Yarotsky's gap constant in `hamiltonian-time`, the kinetic coefficient 1/(2κ) in `joint-cutoff/energy-density`.
3. Generator normalization inside A4's lane: iσ_j in `wilson-variance`, iσ_j/2 in the Hamiltonian notes; no factor given.
4. A4's uncoupled-loop survival condition (γah → c) and thin-loop vanishing condition (h(1+β) → 0) use couplings with no stated relation.
5. D4's strong-coupling statement (all connected autocorrelations vanish) and the surviving bands (D4, C4) concern disjoint regimes; no note says so.
6. Declaration counts for B4's `JointCutoff.lean` differ between notes (12 at one revision, 14 at a later one).
7. Lean versions: D4 uses 4.24.0 for three files, the rest 4.19.0; D4's files cannot be replayed on the 4.19.0 hosts as pinned.
8. G4's slice labels are dimensionless and are not related to B4's time step or to physical time.

None of these is a contradiction.

## 5. What going forward would have to mean

Not a plan adopted by anyone; the choices this gathering leaves.

1. For Yang–Mills the fixed-mesh programme is close to saturated: further constants at fixed mesh do not move requirement (a) or (e). Work that would move them has to face the spatial refinement: a block or flow observable of fixed physical size, a renormalization factor, and an estimate at weak coupling. No lane has such an estimate, and the literature has none in four dimensions.
2. The cheapest decisive checks available are on the two external claims, by replaying the formal developments (a machine with the resources is needed; this one has no such capacity confirmed).
3. The conventions list above can be closed by the directors in one pass.
