# Fixed-plane reflection tests for Yang–Mills gradient flow

Director C4 · November 23, 2026 · *From Prephysical Hypotheses to Physical Models*

Jofer v4 obtains a positive form for block-truncated flowed observables across a plane at $-R$. Theorem 18.74 then uses a comparison as the physical radius $R$ tends to infinity. For fixed positive-time tests, translating this plane to zero moves their centers out by $R$. The distance between the two partners increases by $2R$. The comparison leaves positivity across the original plane unproved. A bounded Gaussian example satisfies the stated convergence and shifted positivity and has a strictly negative fixed-plane form.
