## Numerical and original-link controls

The [original-link check](check_links.py) evaluates genuine ordered quaternion products for both packets and every marked edge of a finite straight trunk. Replacing just the trunk changes the normalized angle from 0.392402817 to −0.512041263 while both exterior endpoint vectors remain fixed. Independent gauge rotations preserve the angle. Explicit four-dimensional plaquette incidence with period five finds four shared faces for each adjacent-time control and zero for each nonadjacent control. One-link Haar quadrature for aligned staples reproduces both spin-one and spin-two Bessel multipliers. At $\beta=2,m=12$, the resulting first and second angular moments are −0.00627943 and 0.33333257. This is an exact conditional test at a fixed exterior.

The [Gaussian calculation](check_angle.py) uses a compact smooth tensor-product bump of coordinate half-width four, translated by ten units. Independent spatial Fourier quadratures give $v+w=0.01971888303$, $v-w=0.01974025182$ and $w/v=-0.0005415421$. Radial and angular Jacobi quadratures give $b_+=0.3465318405$ and variance 0.3333332369. At physical times 0.3 and 1 the two-mode quadratic lower bounds are 0.0935106 and 0.0127842. Four scrambled Sobol calculations estimate the full covariances as 0.1021381 and 0.0129172 with between-scramble ranges 0.000647 and 0.000069. These are quadrature checks, not certified error bars. An initial numerical assertion imposed an unsupported 0.04 lower bound at both times and failed at time 1. The corrected test uses the calculated lower bound at each time, checks positivity and doubles the sample size. The failed log is preserved. The angular threshold check recovers the extra two energy powers for the minus mode.

The [finite reversible transfer control](check_time.py) evaluates the exact consecutive-step conditional variance and characteristic function of a bounded five-state process. At one finite mixing parameter its one- and two-step covariances are 0.0700 and 0.0147. For the trajectory in this control, the error of the time-sum characteristic function against the Brownian prediction falls from 0.00838 at 40 slices to 0.000525 at 640. This checks normalization and distinguishes the two time separations. The analytical Wilson application uses the original two-trunk bound.
