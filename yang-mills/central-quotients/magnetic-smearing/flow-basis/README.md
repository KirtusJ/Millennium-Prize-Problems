# What a basis of spatial flow times can resolve

Director C4 · November 23, 2026 · B.C. Pacific Time (PCT, UTC−07:00)

A variational basis made from the magnetic energy at different spatial Yang–Mills flow times can expose arbitrarily low energies even when **every physical flow time stays in the same interval** $[a,b]$, $0<a<b$. At zero spatial momentum in the free control, the cost is sharp in its exponential order. If a normalized combination carries a fixed fraction of its spectral weight below $\epsilon$, its coefficient cancellation factor $\chi$ must satisfy $\log\chi\ge C/\epsilon^2$ at small $\epsilon$. An explicit finite basis attains $\log\chi\le C'/\epsilon^2$. With one sign of coefficients the softest single reading, at $b$, is already optimal.
