### Proposed proofs and a quantitative comparison

**Jofer.** The proof of v2 Lemma 18.70 (p. 121 and following) and v3 Lemma 18.71 (pp. 133–134) claims full Wilson flow preserves the positive-half form for every bounded functional of nominally positive-time flowed links. It invokes reflection equivariance and locality. A finite-time parabolic map can commute with reflection while depending on original negative-time links. The exact compact $U(1)$ test in this folder contradicts the unqualified compact-group step. The written $SU(2)$ argument supplies a simple-group test under the program's stated microscopic inputs. Both versions use the lemma in their subsequent fixed-flow OS construction. This disposition concerns that inference; it neither decides another version by association nor rules out a different construction. [Jofer v2, §18.7](https://www.ai.vixra.org/pdf/2509.0025v2.pdf) · [Jofer v3, §18.7](https://www.ai.vixra.org/pdf/2509.0025v3.pdf)

**Eriksson.** The February 2026 manuscript distinguishes the original Wilson half-algebra from a flowed algebra. Its Theorem 4.4 concerns an approximate inequality at positive buffer distance. Reconstruction on the flowed algebra is explicitly conditional on exact positivity. The signed tests here are compatible with such a distinction. We have not verified the manuscript's claimed uniform Gaussian localization or its continuum inputs. [Almost Reflection Positivity for Gradient-Flow Observables, §§3–5](https://ai.vixra.org/pdf/2602.0084v1.pdf)

**Faizal and Shabir.** The publisher records an article claiming a reflection-positive continuum construction and confinement. A manuscript copy bearing the same authors and title gives a testable comparison in Theorem 10.8, Eqs. (10.78)–(10.81), pp. 125–127. With $d_k$ the difference between two trajectories' readings, the displayed bound is $d_{k+1}\le d_k+C\varepsilon_k$, $\sum\varepsilon_k<\infty$. This bounds $d_K$ by $d_0+C\sum_{k<K}\varepsilon_k$; it does not force $d_K\to0$. Even $d_0=0$, $\varepsilon_0=1$, $\varepsilon_{k>0}=0$ permits $d_{k\ge1}=1$. Adjusting the starting scale needs a separate estimate making the mismatch at that scale vanish. The text also drops positive remainders between insertions because they kill the vacuum. For vacuum $(1,0)$, remainder $\operatorname{diag}(0,1)$ and the coordinate-swap insertion $J$, one has $D\Omega=0$ but $\langle\Omega,JDJ\Omega\rangle=1$. Both counterexamples are Lean-checked. They test the stated implications, without claiming the toy operators realize the Yang–Mills trajectories. An independent estimate comparing the two actual trajectories could change the disposition. The publisher exposed the abstract; the detailed equation numbers refer to the inspected manuscript copy. Equality of that copy and the publisher's full version was not established. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1002/prop.70097) · [Manuscript copy, Theorem 10.8](https://borowiecki.dev/pdf/2606.19362)

The operative next comparison is the original **spatial-flow** point or spatial-volume magnetic reading on a trajectory whose physical scale is fixed independently. Record group, action, boundary conditions, flow prescription, scale convention, momentum projection, operator normalization and order of limits together. For a positive measure with total mass $C(0)$, a measured $C(t)>e^{-tE_*}C(0)$ gives raw mass at least $[C(t)-e^{-tE_*}C(0)]/[1-e^{-tE_*}]$ below or at $E_*$. Passing this test would establish spectral weight for that operator. A positive gap requires exclusion of lower energies throughout the physical Hilbert space.
