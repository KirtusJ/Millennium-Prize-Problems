# Wilson curvature for compact groups and a charge-odd spectral test

Director C4 · November 23, 2026 · B.C. Pacific Time (PCT, UTC−07:00) · Yang–Mills existence and mass gap

For a fixed compact connected gauge group with a Wilson character action, the local weak-coupling curvature has one Gaussian Maxwell color per visible Lie-algebra dimension. The representation fixes the color metric. Two gauge-invariant measurements detect the Lie bracket beyond the leading linear cube identity. For the ordinary imaginary $SU(3)$ plaquette trace, charge conjugation isolates a separate spectral sector.

**At fixed lattice cells**, first take the original Wilson transfer vacuum on each spatial torus, then let $\beta,L\to\infty$ at any relative rates. For disjoint coordinate faces $p=12$, $q=34$ at one site, the scaled Wilson action $\beta^2 V(U_pU_qU_p^{-1}U_q^{-1})$ has limiting mean $dc/8$ and variance divided by squared mean $7/d$ for a simple observable algebra of dimension $d$ and adjoint Casimir $c$ in the Wilson metric. The ordered six-face cube has a signed third moment whose coefficient at order $\beta^{-1/2}$ is negative. Dividing its coefficient by the commutator mean removes the group normalization. On a single actual $SU(3)$ face, $\beta^{3/2}\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Tr}(U_p)/3$ has limiting variance $5/12$. These local results also apply when the bare coupling is chosen logarithmically in a separate scale parameter.

**At fixed physical size and positive physical time**, the explicit rapidly weakening trajectories extend to each fixed compact simple visible group. For example take inverse spacing $r\to\infty$, $\beta=r^{17}$ and $L=\lceil r^4\rceil$. Smoothly smeared invariant quadratic, cubic and quartic operators have connected physical spectral masses of orders $\epsilon^8$, $\epsilon^{12}$ and $\epsilon^{16}$ near zero. A single positive-separation determinant divided by the square of the cubic reading tends to three. In the fundamental $SU(3)$ theory a charge-odd and charge-even cubic have a positive-time ratio of $5/9$ and exactly zero mixed reading at every regulator. The odd physical gap tends to zero along these free trajectories. For fixed physical spacing determined by a nonzero interaction scale the Yang–Mills existence and mass-gap problem remains open.
