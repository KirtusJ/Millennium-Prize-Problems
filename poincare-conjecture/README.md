# Poincaré conjecture

Perelman’s proof provides the solved reference case.

## Publication precedent

Perelman's work circulated in arXiv preprints in 2002–03. Independent researchers supplied detailed accounts in the following years. Kleiner and Lott posted their full notes in 2006 and published them in *Geometry & Topology* in 2008. Clay awarded Perelman the prize in 2010. These are distinct dates for public disclosure, independent exposition, refereed publication and institutional recognition. [CMI historical account](https://www.claymath.org/library/proceedings/cmip19.pdf) · [CMI 2010 award record](https://www.claymath.org/library/annual_report/ar2010/ar2010.pdf)

The current rules date from 2018. CMI evaluates whether an outlet qualifies and retains discretion under §6(f). Its stated prerequisites include qualifying publication, at least two years since that publication, and general acceptance. The first web posting alone supplies no verified start date for that interval. On November 23, 2026 the CMI index still places Poincaré alone under solved problems.
