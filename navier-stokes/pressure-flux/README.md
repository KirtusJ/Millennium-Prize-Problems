# The pressure estimate used in whole-space uniqueness

Director A4 · November 22, 2026 research session · B.C. Pacific Time (PCT, UTC−07:00)

The original comparator proof for forced alternative (C) uses a pressure bound that explicitly retains the localized dissipation. Equation (10.19) in the paper gives a different bound. Following the caller resolves how both can lead to the same uniqueness conclusion. The formal proof absorbs the additional term in the difference-energy inequality. This is a source comparison of one intermediate step. [Paper, Lemma 10.5, printed p. 123](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/32d9f210-8b73-45e0-91bc-82a30aef8a9a/navier-stokes.pdf) · [Bastounis, Circelli and Hansen, §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08144v1#S3.SS2)

## Comparing the same pressure flux

For the compact reference velocity and a smooth finite-energy competitor, let $w$ be their difference and $\pi$ the pressure difference with the corresponding sign. The absolute flux $|\int \pi w\cdot\nabla\chi_R|$ is invariant under reversing both differences. With $\chi_R=\phi_R^8$, write $A_R=(\int\phi_R^8|\nabla w|^2)^{1/2}$ and $B_R=\|\phi_R^4w\|_6$. Constants below are uniform in the radius and the interior time on a fixed comparison slab. [Norms in the source](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/NavierStokes/R3/ComparisonSetup.lean#L41) · [Uniform formal bound](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/NavierStokes/R3/PressureFlux.lean#L576)

| Estimate, omitting its constant | Right-hand side |
|---|---|
| Paper (10.19) | $R^{-1}(B_R+1) B_R^{1/2}+R^{-7/4} B_R^{3/4}$ |
| Formal physical-flux theorem | $(B_R^{1/2}+1)(A_R/R+R^{-2})+R^{-7/4} B_R^{3/4}$ |

The second term agrees. The difference lies in the first one. The available Sobolev bound gives an **upper** bound on $B_R$ in terms of $A_R$ and a constant times $1/R$. It supplies no upper bound on $A_R$ in terms of $B_R$. At $R=B_R=1$ the two scalar envelopes are $3$ and $2A_R+3$. For every proposed comparison constant $C\ge0$, choosing $A_R=2C$ respects $B_R\le A_R+1$ and gives $2A_R+3>3C$. This disproves deriving the printed envelope by that scalar comparison alone. It makes no assertion that a solution violates the printed estimate. [Sobolev step](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/NavierStokes/R3/WholeSpaceComparisonClosure.lean#L90) · [Lean control](PressureFlux.lean)

## Where the formal estimate goes

1. At the pinned revision, `actual_flux_eq_canonicalCutoffFlux` identifies the physical integral with the real part of a canonical pairing. The proof bounds its absolute value by the complex norm, bounds the nine tensor-component pairings and obtains one constant valid for every $R\ge1$ and interior time. The hypotheses include uniform $L^2$ control of $w$, $L^3$ control of the compact reference, and $L^1$ control of the components of the quadratic tensor difference. [Physical pairing](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/NavierStokes/R3/PressureFlux.lean#L210) · [Canonical estimate](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/NavierStokes/R3/PressureFlux.lean#L516) · [Uniform estimate](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/NavierStokes/R3/PressureFlux.lean#L576)
2. `classical_uniqueness_on_Icc` constructs these bounds from compact support, smoothness and finite kinetic energy. It passes the pressure theorem directly to `eq_of_pressure_flux_bound`. That theorem uses the weighted Sobolev bound and the localized energy balance. Young absorption in `exists_uniform_rate_bound` yields $E'_R\le 2G E_R+D/R$ with constants independent of $R$. Zero initial difference, Grönwall and the limit of the spatial cutoffs yield equality on the slab. [Caller](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/NavierStokes/R3/WholeSpaceUniqueness.lean#L45) · [Energy closure](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/NavierStokes/R3/WholeSpaceComparisonClosure.lean#L60) · [Scalar rate estimate](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/NavierStokes/R3/ComparisonRateBound.lean#L127)
3. `candidate_global_agrees_before_one` applies the comparison to every closed slab ending before the blowup time. `no_global_solution_one` combines that agreement with the compactly supported candidate’s unbounded velocity before time one and the competitor’s boundedness on the corresponding compact space-time set. The viscosity scaling then gives the arbitrary-positive-viscosity theorem used by the whole-space comparator.
